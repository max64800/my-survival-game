## 2026-11-28 — Nov 28 play test fixes, part 2 (F952–F968, F956, F962, F966)

**What we did**
- **F952:** monster reach is now measured to the middle of your foot and shell, not to your centre point.
- **F953–F955:** the three smaller items from that list.
- **F959:** fixed weapons that were worn but never drawn.
- **F968:** the old ruin hall's broken block walls are solid now, every block and every fallen block. `RuinKit.broken_wall` builds the blocks into a StaticBody3D with a box shape each.
- **F961:** the glowing crest crystals on spiders and bats now sit on the middle of their back, sized to each creature.
  - `MonsterModel._back_top` finds the highest point near the middle of the body on each model.
- **F967:** bats and beetles stopped losing track of you.
  - Bats with rock between them and you no longer use up the pack's dive turn. After 1 s blocked they fly a new, tighter circle.
  - Beetles step onto low rocks, and climb over tall ones after pushing on them for 0.8 s.
  - Beetles no longer charge rocks that are in the way.
- **F962 (came back from F927):** while aiming, the camera arm stops tipping at a gentle angle, and only the view keeps tilting up, to about 83° (it stopped at 63° before). The camera stays behind him instead of swinging down into his shoulder.
- **F966:** copper and tin ore get chunky metal chips gathered in bands. Copper is orange with a green-blue crust, tin is silver-grey. The ore look photos (`lookdev ore`) now also visit the Copper Deeps.
- **F956:** a new bolt wheel (`src/ui/bolt_wheel.gd`).
  - Hold R with the crossbow loaded and a ring of your bolt types opens. Point with the mouse and let go of R to pick one.
  - A tap of R still reloads an empty crossbow.
  - The host still checks that you carry the bolt you shoot.
- The owner asked for a separate chat for the map and world-shape work (F969–F974). Claude wrote the handoff prompt.

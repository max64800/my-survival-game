# Twin Gate: a page that explains the new pieces (Nov 21)

**What we did**
- The owner asked which existing pieces the turrets and short battlements were made from. Answer: none, each is one
  new piece. Made a second 3D page (tools/ruins/twin_gate_turret_page.html): the turret and the short battlement on
  their own and in place, a "Build it up" slider that grows them row by row, and buttons that light up the known pieces
  around them.
- Explained how the buttresses are built (side outline pushed out sideways, sliced into 10 rows, rows split like brickwork).
- Owner OK'd the Twin Gate and asked for many more roofs and some balconies on the next ruins (noted in F479).

**Mistakes and hurdles**
- The first screenshot showed the new pieces glowing far too blue, the "All four" view cut off the right station, and
  the "loading" text never went away (the page's own style beat the hidden flag when run locally). Fixed all three
  before publishing.
- The browser helper still wouldn't connect; headless Chrome took the screenshot instead.

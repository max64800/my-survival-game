## Nov 21: Hearth Manor pieces, owner's first review of the test house

The owner spotted 3 things on the little test house: (1) two brackets sat right over the round door, (2) there was
no way up to the upper floor, (3) the left side had no trim band. They also asked that brackets snap like other
pieces: to 3 spots on each 2 m wall (both ends and the middle), not at random-looking quarter points.
Fixed: brackets now at the wall ends and middles (one bracket where two walls meet), the ground door moved to the
side so no bracket hangs over it (a rule for the manor too), outside stairs climb to the balcony's open left end,
trim bands on both sides with corner blocks. Claude's mistake: placing brackets without checking what was under them.

## Nov 28 — F981: the Iron King, the new main boss (design + moves test)

**What we did:** The owner wanted a new main boss: a giant armoured dwarf ghost-king. Later he gets a sunken throne
hall and an army of skeleton soldiers. We started with the boss only.
- **Picture first:** ChatGPT drew 3 versions on one sheet (`assets/concepts/iron_king/iron_king_v1.png`). The owner
  picked **A**: a tall spiky iron crown and a greataxe.
- **Owner's picks (multiple choice):**
  - Solid iron armour with a green ghost glow inside.
  - About 2.6 m tall.
  - 3 phases:
    1. Hammer + shield: Shield Bash, Shield Charge, Hammer Slam.
    2. At 60% health he breaks his shield and swaps to the greataxe: Chop and Spin.
    3. At 25%, "ghost rage": he gets faster, his axe leaves fire rings on the floor, and he can fade away and rush back.
  - His ghost fade comes in phase 3 only.
  - In co-op he gets more health and is harder to stun.
- **Old bosses deleted:** the design notes for Orrumhal (Zone 1) and Thallus (Fungal Jungle) are gone, with the owner's OK.
  Every page that named them now points to the Iron King. The Fungal Jungle boss is "not chosen yet".
  The full design is in `docs/design/combat-and-monsters.md` 8.2.
- **Easy moves (the owner's main wish):** "Keep the moves, swap the body". The Iron King IS a dwarf model, scaled to 2x.
  It has the dwarf's own skeleton and the attack moves the owner already OK'd: the shield bash, the mace's smash,
  the Foreman's greataxe chop and spin. The only new one is `Shield_Dash`, from the same free pack.
  So there's no copying of moves onto a new skeleton. That copying (a "retarget") caused all the old animation trouble.
- **Moves test** (`-- ironking`): a test world where he walks up to you and does all 5 attacks in turn. Each attack's
  name floats over his head, and his weapon glows green during the wind-up (the warning).
  Files: `src/combat/iron_king.gd` and `src/debug/iron_king_show.gd`.
  For now he wears the bronze plate armour painted darker as a stand-in. His own hand-built armour comes next.

**Mistakes and hurdles:**
- **The whole king was one white glow** in the first pictures. I painted the "ghost inside" on everything that wasn't
  armour. But the armour pieces get moved onto the skeleton when he puts them on, so my check found no armour at all,
  and the ghost paint (made for see-through ghosts) went over everything.
  Fix: remember every part *before* he dresses; the new parts after dressing are the armour. The ghost inside is now
  a dark paint with a soft green glow instead of the see-through ghost paint.
- **The Shield Charge ran into the camera.** He charged 5 m from where he stood, right through the player.
  Fix: he steps back to 9 m first, turns round, and the charge stops 2.5 m in front of you.
- **"Could not find type IronKing":** Godot didn't know the new class name yet. Fix: re-import the project once
  (`--headless --import`).
- **My own dwarf blocked the pictures.** Fix: in robot picture runs the player's model is hidden.
- **Still open:** the "iron" paint still looks bronze-brown (his own armour will fix it). Godot also prints
  "material is null" 32 times while he loads; it does no harm, but I didn't find out why.
- **Shared files:** `main.gd` also held another chat's unsaved Brickcrown lines, so only my `ironking` lines were
  committed (with a hand-made patch). I didn't touch `ghost.gd` (another chat is changing it).

**Next:** the owner looks at the moves. Then, in a new chat: his own hand-built armour (spiky crown, iron plates,
cloak, mist beard, a real warhammer), then the fight brain, then the hall and the army.

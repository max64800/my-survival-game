## Nov 25: F861 the slit between two rows of a wooden steep roof

**What we did**
- The owner sent a picture from inside a wooden steep roof: a long band of cave rock showing between two roof rows.
  The F865 fix (the real roof model shows again instead of the plain code shape) had made this older problem visible.
- Cause: `shack_v2.py` built the roofs with `K.Slope(..., top_v=0.33)`, so the panel climbed only (rise − 0.33) over
  its length: 1.5 m instead of 2 m on a steep piece. Each row ended about 40 cm lower than the next row started.
- Fix: the 6 Shack sloped roofs (steep, gentle, both corner kinds) now climb the full rise (top_v 0). The shingles run
  up to under the top log, and a hidden dark board under the high edge and the +x side covers the 1 cm cracks
  between rows and between neighbours. The shingles now reach about 25 cm above the piece's box at the top edge.
  That's on purpose: it's the start of the next row's space, and a straight roof line needs it.
- The steep inner corner now starts 16 cm up, so its `model_lift` is 0.2. The new check from F865 caught that it
  would have been thrown out.

**Mistakes and hurdles**
- F865 was called done without looking at two rows together. Fixing the model's fit made the gap between rows show.
  Lesson: after making a roof model show, look at a stacked roof (two rows) from inside before calling it done.
- Blender crashed (access violation) on the second picture in one run. One picture per run worked.
- Brick roofs have a small 6 cm version of the same gap. They were left alone, because another chat has open,
  uncommitted work in the Hearthbrick roof scripts.

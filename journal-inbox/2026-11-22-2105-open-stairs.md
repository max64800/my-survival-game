## Nov 22 (PC, night): open stairs everywhere, and tower stairs you can really climb (F169 🔁, F626 🔁)

**What we did**
- Remade every straight and corner stairs (cut stone, clay brick, wood) as just the steps on a thin slanted
  underside, open below, so you can walk under them or put a chest there. Showed old and new side by side on a
  3D page first; the owner OKed it ("the new just normal stairs is great, implement it everywhere").
- The stairs' invisible shapes now match the new models (`PieceMesh.STAIRS_UNDER`, `CORNER_UNDER`), and a new
  `PieceMesh.stair_underside` tells how much room there is under any spot of a stairs.
- Building rules: `Structure.fits_under_stairs` lets a chest, barrel, crate or table stand in that open space (also
  when the furniture was there first). A tall shelf still doesn't fit. Test: `test_open_stairs`.
- Hearth Manor tower: the spiral stairs became 3 ordinary straight flights, and the pyramid roof on the lookout
  became a flat deck with railings (the owner asked for it, so it skips the "Hearthbrick roofs are sloped" rule).
- Twin Gate towers: straight + corner stairs instead of the spiral ones.
- New test `test_dwarf_walks_up_every_tower_flight` builds both towers and walks a test dwarf up every flight.
- The wooden corner stairs had one step board floating 5 cm above its stone (old bug); the stone is now tall enough.
- Still open (F716): the spiral stairs piece you build yourself is hard to climb and ends in empty air at the top.

**Hurdles and mistakes**
- The 3D page wouldn't take `.glb` files, so the models went in as glTF text. Then the page broke ("Failed to load
  buffer data:...") because the page isn't allowed to fetch the data inside itself. Fix: the page rebuilds each
  model in memory and hands it straight to the loader. The owner saw the broken page first.
- The first camera views were confusing (from the back the new stairs still looked solid). Moved the camera round
  so you look into the open space.
- The browser helper kept timing out, and port 8765 was another chat's page server. Used a headless Edge
  screenshot and a different port instead.
- `run_tests.sh` failed at first because Godot isn't on the PC's PATH; it needs `GODOT` set to the winget Godot.
- The Twin Gate corner stairs walk test failed: the dwarf got stuck on the second step. First guess: the step-up
  hop (50 cm) hits the floor above, so let it try a smaller hop. That didn't help, and the real reason was simpler:
  the corner stairs' tall steps start right at the edge of the hole in the floor above, so a dwarf's head has no
  room there at all. Fix: open one more floor tile above the corner stairs' foot (with railings round it), and the
  hop change was undone.
- Several files were also being changed by other chats (a bevel pass on the brick builder, the bartizan in
  `piece_mesh.gd`, `structure.gd`), so only this chat's lines were committed. The rebuilt brick stairs models
  already include the other chat's bevelled bricks.
- The Twin Gate ruin test (another chat's unfinished work, not in git yet) fails before it reaches any stairs.
- The owner asked: "do you have to build this little platform under the stairs?" Every blueprint flight stood on
  an extra floor slab (to make its top step level with the floor above). With open stairs that slab became a
  25 cm ledge you had to jump onto. Fix: `BlueprintLibrary._stairs_on_the_floor` takes those slabs out and sets
  each flight straight on the floor; its top is now one ordinary step below the next floor.
- That pass removes pieces, which broke another chat's wooden Prow Hall code (it matches the stone hall piece by
  piece, by number). Fix: for the wooden Prow Hall the slab pass runs before that step.
- The owner rebuilt the Manor and found round stairs AND straight stairs side by side, the straight ones blocked
  by a closed floor. Cause: each ruin has a saved "ruin plan" (which pieces lie around broken), and the Manor's
  was made from the old blueprint. Rebuilding adds the new pieces but keeps the old plan's leftovers. Fix: made the
  Manor, Brewhall, Kiln, Outpost, Spire and Watchtower ruin plans again from the new blueprints. Lesson: whenever
  a ruin's blueprint changes, its ruin plan must be made again too. (The Prow Hall and Twin Gate plans belong to
  chats that are changing them right now, so they make theirs again themselves.) Old saved worlds keep the old Manor.
- Another chat's journal commit swept up all of this chat's staged files by mistake; that chat then redid its
  commit with only its own file, which emptied this chat's staging. Fix: put the staged files back from the
  swept commit (`git restore --source=<that commit> --staged`), without touching anyone's working files.
- Two "the ruin lands on the same spot" checks (Manor, Spire) and the old watchtower tests fail because another
  chat is changing the cave maker right now; the stairs work doesn't touch the cave.

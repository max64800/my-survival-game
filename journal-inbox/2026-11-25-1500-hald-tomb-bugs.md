## Nov 25: three Tomb of Hald bugs from the owner's play test (F846, F847, F848)

**What we did**
- **F846, dying at full health before the boss room.** The spike pit under the Broken Stair has an "if you are down
  in here, you die" check. That check had a top (1.2 m over the pit's floor) but **no bottom**. The tomb door and the
  north edge of Hald's Tomb lie 5 m straight under the pit, so walking through them counted as "in the pit". Fix:
  the check now also needs you to be no more than 2 m under the pit's floor (`DungeonBuild.deadly_space`).
  The stairs over the pit were never the problem; the room under it was.
- **F847, invisible walls and platforms on the stairs.** Same cause as last chat's doorway bug: a dungeon model with
  no collision boxes of its own gets one big box round the whole model. Hald's 20 stone stairs had none, so each was a
  solid 2 m block: you stood on air on its top and bumped its sides. Fix: in a dungeon, every `stairs__*` piece now
  gets the same slanted slope the stairs you build get (`PieceNode.stairs_slope`).
- **F848, the boss room's door didn't open.** The Bronze door behind Hald's coffin opens with the Bronze Key. The lock
  code (`DungeonKeys.ask_unlock`) was written long ago, but **nothing ever called it**: E on the door did nothing.
  Hald is the first dungeon with a key door, so nobody had tried it. Fix: E on a key door now shows
  "Locked door (the Bronze Key) · E to unlock" and asks the host to unlock it.
- New tests in `tests/test_hald.gd`: `test_over_and_under_the_spike_pit_is_safe`, `test_walk_up_every_stair`,
  `test_the_bronze_door_opens_with_its_key` (the Shield-Brother test stays last).

**Mistakes and hurdles**
- The handoff guessed the pit's kill box reached *up* to the stairs. It reached *down* instead. Reading the map's
  numbers (the pit and the tomb door share the same x/z) found it.
- The handoff guessed the boss door "listens for the room being won". It doesn't: it is a key door, and the E button
  was never joined to it.
- My first stairs test failed 6 times: the test's "dwarf ball" at the very top of each flight touched the real wall
  beside the doorway at the top. Not a bug in the game; the check now stops 60 cm before the top.
- `git pull` was blocked by other chats' unsaved files (CREDITS.md, src/main.gd). Another chat also had unsaved lines
  in `dungeon_build.gd`, so I committed only my own lines (a patch of just my parts), never `git stash`.

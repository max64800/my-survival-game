## Nov 28 (night, later): the dwarf's eyes look one way and sit less deep (F943)

**What the owner said (2 pictures):** "we also had a problem with his eyes" (picked "looking different ways" from the
choices), then "they are too deep now".

**What we did:** in `dwarf_head.py` each eye's look line pointed 8% outward (`eye_look`: `s * 0.08`), so the left eye
looked left and the right eye right. Now both turn 3% inward (`EYE_TOE = -0.03`), so they meet about 1.5 m in front of him.
The eyeballs sit 6 mm further forward (`EYE_FWD = 0.066`, was 0.06). The lids follow on their own, since they are built
around `eye_centre` and `eye_look`.

**Hurdle:** the look direction was written in three places: `eye_look` for the lids, plus two `eye_attrs` calls that
paint the pupil, each with its own copy of the numbers. Changing one would have left the pupils pointing the old way.
Both calls now use `eye_look`, so there is only one number to change.

**Mistake:** Claude first guessed the new shadow setting (`ao_local`) had changed the eyes. Comparing the old and new
eye paint showed they were almost the same, so it asked the owner what was wrong instead of guessing further.

**Checks:** the build's "rays that reach the eye" stays 5 of 5 on both eyes. test_visuals passes; test_dwarf only
has the old, known failures.

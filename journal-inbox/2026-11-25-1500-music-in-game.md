# 2026-11-25: music in the game

**What we did:** Four rounds of picking music on review pages with the owner (they want quiet solo instruments, like Valheim,
not big orchestras). Their picks are now in the game: `assets/music/` (23 ogg files, 36 MB) and a new `src/music.gd`.
- Calm solo tracks while exploring (with 10-30 s of silence between), darker tracks in caves/dungeons, a drum track in a fight,
  a deep track near a boss. Tracks crossfade over 3 s; one never repeats right away.
- Jingles: horn by ruin size (small = outpost/tower, large = manor/spire, rest medium; table `Music.RUIN_SIZE`),
  chime for a learned recipe (quiet harp for campfire recipes), "The Last Horn" (14 s) when a boss dies. All play on every computer
  (`GameAuthority.music_stinger`, `_ev_stinger`).
- Music slider in Settings, saved with the other settings (`SoundSettings.music_volume`, its own "Music" bus made in code).
- `tests/test_music.gd`; CREDITS.md Music section.

**Hurdles:** the review page's seek bar jumped back (browser can't seek a streamed mp3): fixed with blob URLs. ffmpeg here has no
libvorbis (used `-c:a vorbis -strict -2`) and a cover-art mp3 needed `-vn`. A Python f-string with a backslash broke a script.

**Open:** the boss-defeated sound is a placeholder (owner never loved one); combat music is thin (only "maybe" picks);
no bandura/kobza found in free libraries; there is no credits screen yet, so the CC-BY artists are only in CREDITS.md;
the owner's numbers on some notes (635, 877...) were not used.

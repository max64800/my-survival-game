## Nov 23, 23:45 (PC): new bag window, putting things on takes a moment, Q throws away (F825, F826)

**What we did**
- Settled the open questions with the owner first (godot-grill), then made a mock-up picture
  (`docs/design/mockups/2026-11-23-bag-window.png`). Decisions: `docs/decisions/2026-11-23-bag-and-equip.md`.
- **F825, the bag window:** three panels side by side. Left = a new **Gear** panel with big slots and the item's
  name beside each (head, chest, legs, feet, lantern, backpack). Middle = the bag. Right = crafting. The owner asked
  for no dwarf outline for now, so it's two neat columns. On small screens all three shrink together to fit.
- **Weapons, shields and pickaxes are now worn where they lie, like Valheim.** Right-click one in the hotbar or bag and
  it gets a gold **E**. Only one of each kind is worn. The old pickaxe/weapon/shield slots are gone from the screen.
  Old saves move whatever was in them into the bag, still worn. A friend who joins later arrives with a worn pickaxe.
- **F826, equip time:** putting something on fills a gold ring around its slot. Weapons, tools and shields take
  0.5 s; armour takes 1.5 s. While it fills you walk at 30% speed and your hands are empty. A hit stops it. Taking
  things off is instant. The ring runs on the player's own computer (that's where hits land), then the host does the change.
- **Q over a slot throws it on the floor.** The item keeps its wear and upgrades when you pick it back up (also through a save).
- New test robot `bagcheck` (`-- bagcheck claude=bagui`) photographs the bag, the ring and a throw.
- 5 new tests (ring timing + a hit stopping it, Q-throw keeping data, a host throw making drops, old saves moving out).

**Mistakes and hurdles**
- Long Bash heredocs broke twice ("unexpected EOF"). Writing the script to a file first and running it fixed that.
- The tests wouldn't start: `godot` isn't on the PATH in this shell. Setting `GODOT=` to the WinGet path fixed it
  (that path holds the username, so it is never committed).
- My mock-up put the hotbar inside the bag, but the owner's F241 keeps the hotbar at the bottom of the screen. The
  real build keeps it there.
- I found a bug in my own `set_worn`: with an old save's sword still in the old weapon slot, putting on a new sword
  would have deleted the new one. Now the two trade places.
- The first game photo showed the crafting panel running off the right edge (gear + bag + crafting is too wide for
  a 1600-wide window). Fixed by shrinking all panels together when they don't fit.
- **Q-throw looked broken in the game but worked in the test.** The thrown item landed 1.5 m in front of the dwarf,
  inside his 4 m pickup magnet, so it flew straight back into the bag. Now a thrown drop waits until you've walked
  away from it once.
- While renaming a variable in the robot script with `sed`, I missed one spot twice and started a 2-minute game load
  each time for nothing. Lesson: grep the file before re-running the game.
- An axe can't be equipped (it isn't a pickaxe, weapon or shield), so the robot's first ring photo showed nothing.
  It now uses a stone sword.

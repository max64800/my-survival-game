## Nov 26: Copper armour, round 4 (F914)

**What we did**
- The 3D page now walks with the game's own walk. The game dwarf was recorded walking, and every bone's turn was copied onto the page dwarf (no more T-pose walking).
- "Glued together" fix: every chest band is now its own stiff copper plate with a thick edge. It's laid over a very smooth copy of his body (not over his lumps), overlaps the band below, and its bottom edge stands out, so it throws a real shadow. The cloth under the plates is dark, so the gaps read as deep shadow. 3 skirt rows, one rivet per plate. Shoulder guards are 4 bands stepping down the arm.
- "Helmet too round" fix: a tall dome rising to a point, a band over the top, a riveted brow band, a nose plate, one eye slit, and a bell-shaped guard flaring over the neck and cheeks.
- Redder, brighter copper and bigger hammer dents. Taller shin guards, bigger knee cap.
- Page v6: https://claude.ai/artifact/VCGQWjnc9oM23zuAiCZqFo

**Mistakes and hurdles (and fixes)**
- The old page server on port 8765 kept showing an old page; started a new one on 8791.
- Blender isn't on the PC's PATH: run it by its full path.
- A search over a big folder timed out; searched a smaller spot instead.
- Review 1: a big arch hole in the back of the helmet. The helmet was cut away wherever his back hair passed under it. Fix: only cut round the face and beard at the front.
- Review 1: the shoulder caps grew a fin up the neck and had floating crumbs. Fix: an inner limit on the cap, and tiny bits are dropped.
- Thin beard strands punched little holes through the breastplate. Fix: only the beard's main mass dents the plate (a smoothed copy of the beard).
- Claude's mistake: to make the plates "stiff", each chest plate moved as one piece with one body point. On the page (standing pose) his belly bent forward and poked through the breastplate as a white patch. The Blender test picture uses the T-pose, so it didn't show. Fix: the plates bend with the body like the leather under them. Their shapes are still stiff. Lesson: always check armour in the standing/walking pose, not only the T-pose.
- The page copy at 512 px pictures made dark smudges (the paint is thousands of tiny patches; shrinking mixes in the black gaps). Fix: page copy at 1024 px.
- The browser kept old model files with the same names; new file names each time (copper4d_*).

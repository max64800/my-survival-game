# Copper armour round 12 (F914)

**Owner's notes on round 11:** the chest is too wide (it should end closer to the armpits), the hair floats at the back, the copper could be a bit darker, the run on the page is still too short and glitches, and the helmet's eye holes are too big (keep the width, make them thinner and a bit higher).

**What we did:**
- Breastplate narrower: half-width 0.31 m to 0.26 m (10 cm less across).
- Back hair: tucked in 1.2 cm UNDER the back plate, like hair tucked into a collar. Lifting it out over the plate (round 11) left it floating.
- Copper 20% darker: the hammered copper picture is darkened when the build runs, and so is the polished colour.
- Eye holes: 2.2 cm tall instead of 4 cm, same width, top edge kept under the brow band (so they sit higher).
- Page Run: the export assumed the run plays at the old speed (0.7). In the game it plays at 1.3, because the feet grip the floor. So each recording stopped halfway through a step and jumped. It was also recorded at 4 m/s, which is half jog, half sprint. Now it's recorded at exactly the jog speed, with whole loops at the real speed, 4 seconds or more (Walk 4.4 s, Run 4.3 s).

**Mistakes (Claude's):**
- Round 11's "3 loops" used the wrong loop speed, so the run still jumped.
- Round 11 lifted the braids out, so they floated. Tucking them under is what a real collar does.

**Checks:** the armour fit test passes.

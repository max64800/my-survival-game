## 2026-11-25 (PC) — Tin ore in Zone 1's walls (F697, Zone 1 metal plan step 3)

- **Goal:** step 3 of `docs/decisions/2026-11-24-zone1-metal-and-loot.md`: tin ore as a new cave wall rock, so
  bronze (step 4) has its second ingredient. Claimed F697 in FEEDBACK.md first.
- **Followed copper's path:** copper is rock number 3 in `src/world/mat.gd`; the cave maker decides per 2 m block
  whether a wall spot is ore (`_is_ore`), the terrain shader paints it from baked texture slices, and the authority
  says which pickaxe is strong enough (`ore_too_hard`).
- **Built:**
  - `Mat.TIN = 6` (a free number): hardness 40, needs tier 1 (Copper Pickaxe), drops `tin_ore` (3 bits = 1 ore),
    6 mining XP. New `Mat.is_ore()` so tin also gets "always at least one ore" and the Ore Smasher perk.
  - `ore_too_hard`: stone pickaxe, Fang Pickaxe and bare hands bounce off tin with "Make a Copper Pickaxe at the Anvil".
  - Item `tin_ore` (grey, "Sn"): weight like ore, goes in ore crates and builders, a "where to get it" hint.
  - Cave maker: own noise (`_tin_noise`, seed + 404), only on the Mossy side, a little rare (the test keeps it between 0.2% and 3% of rock), never where
    copper is, never in the start room, and kept 4 m away from everything built (`reserved`: tower, Outpost and the
    other ruins, dungeon doorways and dungeons) and 7 m from a place's middle (camps, shrines, halls). Both the
    one-voxel path and the fast "all rock" path (`_fill_tin`) use it.
  - Look: `bake_terrain_looks.py` paints chunky lopsided grey-silver chips (5-7 sided, light worn rim, dark gap) on
    our 4 painted stone looks; the chips also go in the alpha channel, so the shader gives them a soft metal glint
    (dimmer than copper). Only tin's 4 slices changed (checked pixel by pixel against the old file).
  - Dropped-item model `tin_ore.glb` (Blender, same `ore()` builder as copper/silver, grey stone + dull chips);
    icon = the copper ore icon with its orange turned grey by script. CREDITS.md row added.
- **Tests:** `test_tin_in_mossy_walls` (rare but all around, none in the Jungle), `test_tin_needs_the_copper_pickaxe`,
  and the placement rule test `test_tin_keeps_out_of_built_places` (samples every built place on 3 seeds, and also
  builds the real rock blocks around each one with `generate_block`).
- **Hurdles:**
  1. Copper ore never checked built places at all (a helper found the `reserved` list). Tin checks it.
  2. First test run: tin still showed up at the edges of built places on 2 of 3 seeds. Cause: tin is decided per
     2 m block from the block's middle, so a block whose middle was just outside could still reach inside. Fix: a
     wider margin (4 m) and also looking 2.5 m below and 3 m above the middle.
  3. My own POI test sampled a 10 m cube (corners 8.7 m away) while the rule is 7 m; trimmed the test to 5.5 m.
  4. `run_tests.sh only=test_tin_...` ran nothing: `only=` takes the file name, `fn=` the test name.
  5. A Python script saving to a `/c/...` path failed on Windows: Python needs `C:/...` paths.
  6. CREDITS.md already had another chat's unsaved change: staged only my own line (built the file from HEAD + my
     row with `git hash-object` / `update-index`) so their work isn't committed by me.

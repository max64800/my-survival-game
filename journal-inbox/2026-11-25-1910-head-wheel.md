## 2026-11-25 · F899 step 1: the Head Wheel piece (ruin 12 "Mine-head Works")

**What we did**
- Made a new Shack building piece, the **Head Wheel**: the big wooden wheel on top of a mine's headframe.
  About 4 m across, a 16-sided rim (16 chunky wood blocks) with a rope groove between two cheek rings,
  8 hewn spokes, a round hub log with 2 iron hoops, an 8-sided iron axle with end caps, and two wooden
  bearing blocks with iron straps. Built in Blender by script (`tools/asset_forge/builds/shack_head_wheel.py`),
  in the workbench style, every part sinking ~1 cm into the next (no shimmer). Model checks pass.
- Put it in the game: `pieces.gd` "head_wheel" (Supports, workbench level 2, 12 planks + 2 copper bars).
  New placing rule `hang_on` / `hang_apart`: aim at one beam and the wheel puts one bearing on it and the
  other 4 m away, on the side you aim at, axle turned across the beam. Its see-through ghost and its
  bump shape are a simple disc + axle + 2 blocks (`piece_mesh.gd` `_head_wheel`).
- New test `test_head_wheel_hangs_between_two_beams` (Shack only, real model, copper cost, both turns).
- A 3D page for the owner: https://claude.ai/artifact/6xBaLJ19oHYR7oBn8VkCrB (`tools/build_pieces/head_wheel_page/`),
  the wheel resting on two plain beams 4 m apart. Checked it in a browser myself.

  and put the `elif` back.
- Claude's slip: first version added the lift and took it away again. Simplified to add it only once.

  directly" guard. Icons other chats had changed came out the same; a few new untracked icons appeared

- Render round 1: spokes, hub and axle looked thin next to the chunky rim. Round 2 made them chunkier
  (spoke 7.4 → 9.8 cm, hub 33 → 42 cm, axle 8.5 → 10 cm). That used up the 2 render rounds, so on to the owner.
- `test_builder` failed 9 checks once, then passed on a re-run: another chat was editing files at the same time.
  A "controls.gd failed to compile" message came from another chat's unfinished work, not this change.

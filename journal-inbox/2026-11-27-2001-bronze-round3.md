## 2026-11-27 — F914 bronze armour, round 3: raised knot bands + medallions

**What we did**
- The owner said bronze round 2 was "way too smooth and way too simple". They wanted carving like old dwarf ruins.
- They picked the Nov 26 bronze picture's look: thick raised knot bands, a knot medallion on the chest and back, and deep hammer dents like the copper.
- New picture maker `tools/art/make_bronze_plate.py` makes three sets of pictures (colour, bumps and height):
  - hammered bronze (the copper recipe in bronze colours);
  - a 3-strand plait;
  - a round knot medallion (a ring with four loops, a stepped diamond in the middle, and studs round the rim).
- `copper_plate_armor.py` (metal=bronze) now builds the knots as **real raised metal**, not paint. A fine grid of points sits on the plate, and the height picture pushes each point out (about 5 mm). The band's edges get little walls that go down into the plate, so there is no gap and no shimmer.
- Where the bands are: round the breast and back plates, the shoulder caps, the collar, the helmet's brow band and back skirt, and the forearm cuffs.
- Medallions: two on the breastplate, one each side of the beard's middle braid (the owner's pick), plus one big one in the middle of the back.
- The legs and boots already had copper's deep hammer dents, so they stay as they are (no knot bands yet).
- Deleted the old smooth and engraved bronze pictures, since nothing uses them now.
- Copper is unchanged. I checked this in game shots.
- The fit test passes. Page updated to v29.

**Mistakes and hurdles (including Claude's)**
- **The plait was drawn wrong at first.** Strands were cut in the wrong places. The code picked the "over" strand from all three strands, even ones far away. Fix: only nearby strands get a say.
- **The first medallion looked like a broken spiral.** Its loops crossed the ring at odd angles. Fix: the loops are now centred on the ring, and the strands are thicker.
- **Too many triangles.** The first chest had 171k faces, because I gave the whole relief a full thickness. Fix: the relief only gets walls at its edges (nobody sees under it), and the grid is a bit coarser. The chest is now 108k faces.
- **The medallion looked too dark.** Fix: its floor is now dark bronze, with green patina only in the cuts beside the strands.
- **A Bash heredoc with triple quotes broke** ("unexpected EOF"). Fix: I wrote the patch scripts to files and ran them with python.
- **Searching the whole repo with grep took too long** and had to be stopped. Fix: use the Grep tool, or search only `tools/` and `src/`.
- **The page copy grew from 5 parts to 6,** but the page only loaded 5. It also went over the 64 MB limit for one upload. Fix: the page now loads 6 parts, and I uploaded them in two goes.

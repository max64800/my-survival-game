# Copper armour round 10: helmet front (F914)

**What we did:** The owner said the shoulders were fine and picked "helmet front again". The helmet now has:
- polished cheek guards beside the face opening;
- eye holes set about 1 cm deeper;
- a bottom edge 2 cm shorter;
- a wider riveted strip over the top, like the picture's.

**Mistakes and hurdles:**
- Round 9's thicker face guard was still hard to see, because it was the same hammered copper as the rest of the helmet. Fix: the face guard and cheek plates now use the smooth polished copper, so they stand out like a separate plate.

**Checks:** the armour fit test passes. 10 tiny touch points between the hair and the helmet were found, all hidden inside the face guard.

## Nov 28, late night: F737, new dwarf face 2 (black topknot), rounds 1-3

**What we did:** Started the 3 new grumpy warrior faces. The owner picked a ChatGPT sheet (`assets/concepts/dwarf/dwarf_faces_grumpy_v1.png`) and its top row: black hair in a topknot, a scar through the left eye, heavy brows, a drooping moustache, a pointed beard with 2 braids. `dwarf_head.py` now has a FACES list (`set_face`), `dwarf_body.py` takes `face=<name>` and saves `dwarf_body_<face>.glb`, and the game can load one with `face=topknot` on the command line (`dwarf_rig.gd body_path()`). The picker at new world comes after all faces are OK'd.

**Round 1 → 2 (owner: "very sleepy, not angry; the beard is glued together; same back hair as face 1"):**
- Dark blobs covered the eyes. A debug build without the brows (new `DWARF_SKIP=brows,...` switch) showed the cause: I had made the frown brows *bigger* than face 1's, which were already huge. Fixed with smaller, closer, angry V brows (eyes now 5/5 in the eye check).
- The forehead was too short: hairline raised, cap locks skipped below it.
- One moustache strand dived into the mouth corner: smoothed its depth.
- The scar was too faint: wider and bolder, in shape and paint.
- The beard's middle was melted to the braids: the lock between them is gone and the point is narrower.
- The back now has no long hair at all (short back that ends at the nape).
- A patch script failed once on wrong tab depth; fixed and re-ran.

**Round 2 → 3 (owner: "his middle braid is attached to his shirt; the forearm bracelets look like strips, add gold, like bronze armour with engravings"):**
- The beard point lay on the shirt and moved with the chest (it had no swing bones). It now stands 1 cm further off and gets its own 7 swing bones (`hair_braid_m`, from a new `POINTS` list in dwarf_head.py). My first try reached too far sideways: 138 mesh edges glued it to a side braid (the glue check caught it); a smaller reach fixed it (0).
- The leather wrist wraps became bronze bracers on every face: gold beads on both edges, a gold zigzag cord in a carved groove, punched dots, a gold boss with a ring. Two pieces (`body_hand_cuff_*` and `body_hand_cuff_gold_*`); the gold sinks into the bronze, never on the same surface. **Mistake:** the first full build came out cream-white: only parts with a PAINT recipe get coloured, so I added bronze and gold paints. Then the bronze looked like tan leather on the page, so I darkened it.
- Rebuilt face 1 too. test_dwarf: the 6 failures are the old known ones (stun meter, fist swings, camera), not from this work.

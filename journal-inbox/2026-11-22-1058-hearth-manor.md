## Nov 22, 2026: Ruin 7, the Hearth Manor (F479, F626)

**What we did**
- Made 5 new Hearthbrick pieces (bracket, trim band, trim corner, 1 m railing, roof window) and the whole Hearth
  Manor: a brick manor house on a terrace, with a main hall, a wing with a balcony on brackets, and a lookout tower.
  There is also a wooden version, the ruin (half fallen in), and a 3D page to look at all three.
- The owner reviewed it (F626): "remove the little cap", "make the tower bigger, the spiral stairs can't be used",
  "no small balconies, the dwarf must be able to walk everywhere". Fixed: the tower is now 4×4 rooms with three
  spiral flights in its corners, each opening onto a floor; the balcony is whole floor tiles; no roof cap. A new
  test walks every spiral stair and checks there is floor (and no wall or railing) where you step on and off.
- The owner OK'd it, so it went into the game: its own ruin script (`src/world/ruin_manor.gd`), a spot in one of
  the biggest Mossy caverns about 200 m from the start, rock beetles guarding it, a Rebuilding Chest by its terrace
  steps (planks or clay bricks), both blueprints learned by rebuilding, and a dev menu button "Ruins → Hearth Manor".
- New tests (`tests/test_ruin_manor.gd`): the plan, the blueprint matching the ruin, the spot on 3 worlds (no stone
  column inside it, all its ground kept clear: the placement rule), and a full rebuild in brick.

**Mistakes and hurdles**
- The page was too big to publish (62 MB, the limit is 64 MB). Fix: left the beetle model off the page.
- Feedback number F616 was already taken by another chat; renamed ours to F626 everywhere.
- A 3×3 tower can't get a clean pointed roof without a cap, and the spiral stairs had no room to step off. Fix: 4×4.
- First spot test failed: on 2 of 3 worlds a stone column stood inside the manor (the manor is 37 m long, so it
  often lands on one). Fix: the spot picker now skips any spot with a column under it, and looks at 5 caverns.
- The dev test cave's floor is bumpy, so a 37 m wide building sits 2.8 m below its middle; one check wanted at most
  2.5 m. Fix: very wide ruins allow 3.2 m (the ruin digs out its own space anyway).
- Claude tried to take a screen photo of the test game, but that would have captured the owner's own screen too;
  checked the game's log instead.

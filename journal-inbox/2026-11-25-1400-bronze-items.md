## Nov 25: Bronze arrives: the Bronze Ingot and 13 bronze items (F697 step 4)

**What we did**
- New smelter recipe: 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Tin Ore makes 1 Bronze Ingot. It shows up once you have found tin ore.
- 13 new bronze items, one for each copper piece: helm, mail, greaves, boots, shield, crossbow, spear, axe, sword,
  mace, great axe, great maul and pickaxe. Their anvil recipes are locked. Step 5 puts one page on each dungeon's stand.
- Owner's choice: bronze sits **halfway** between copper and silver. The plan said "1.3× copper", but silver is only
  1.27× copper, so bronze would have beaten silver. Bronze keeps copper's level and adds a small extra (`boost`):
  +15% damage and +5% shield block. Armour: 6/10/7/5. Pickaxe and axe power: 15 (copper 13, silver 17).
- Looks: `tools/art/make_bronze.py` repaints the copper textures and icons gold-brown. The Blender builds now make a
  bronze copy of each copper model. All 14 pass the model checks. Dwarves wearing bronze armour get a bronze colour.
- Test: `tests/test_bronze.gd` (recipe, every item has a model and icon, stats between copper and silver).

**Mistakes and hurdles (and fixes)**
- The first bronze paint (hue +15°) looked olive green. +12° gives the gold-brown we wanted.
- The model check found two surfaces in the same place on the spear (the shaft ran through the metal socket) and on
  the boots (the two boot tops crossed). These problems were already in the copper and silver versions. Fixed for bronze
  only. Rebuilding the old ones waits for the owner's OK.
- Mistake (Claude): my first "claim the to-do" commit also swept in two files another chat had staged
  (`dungeon_build.gd`, `test_hald.gd`). Undone within a minute. From then on I committed through a separate scratch
  index (`GIT_INDEX_FILE`), so only my files went in.
- `git pull` had to wait. The incoming changes touch `CREDITS.md` and `dungeon_build.gd`, which other chats were
  still editing, so merging then could have mixed up their work. My commits stay local until those chats are done.
- The full test run caught a real bug: I had locked the bronze recipes the same way as copper ("ruin"). But random
  ruin pages pick from every "ruin" recipe, so a random page could have taught Bronze Mail early. Bronze now has its
  own lock, "dungeon": only its own named page teaches it (test added).
- Full run, failures that aren't bronze: `test_needs_pickaxe_selected` and `test_ember_sword_burns` put a sword in
  the hotbar without putting it on. Since F826 a weapon only works once it's on. Also the Foreman's attack count,
  `RuinJunk.shown`, and Jolt running out of bodies late in the run. Left for the owner/other chats.
- Auto-smelting does not make bronze on purpose: it would quietly use up copper ingots you stored.

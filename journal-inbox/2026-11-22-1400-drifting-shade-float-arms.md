## Nov 22, 2026: the Drifting Shade's float arms, and a soda-can mistake (F642, Claude 3)

**What we did**
- The owner didn't like the arms swaying while it floats. They wanted them to go back a little and stay there,
  "like an anime run, but more gracefully". Now, as it glides at you, its arms ease back behind it and hold still, its
  chin lifts a touch, and the arms come forward again as it slows, ready to swing (`ghosts_page.html: trail()`).
  Measured on the page: the hands sit 12 to 22 cm behind the shoulders mid-glide. The owner: "looks good right now".
- Details: the owner asked to "amplify them ten times". Claude made 260 deep dents, 60 scratches, 26 cape folds and a
  big lumpy wave in the cloth. The owner: "he seems like a crunched soda can". Undone.
- Tried instead: tidy, made-by-hand details, like the mace's bands and studs:
  - a rolled hem with running stitches along the cape's edge;
  - a band of little stitched runes above it;
  - cloth strips wrapped three times round each forearm;
  - round studs in the rope belt.
  Waiting for the owner's look.

**Mistakes and hurdles**
- Claude's mistake: "amplify ten times" was read as "ten times more damage". Lots of random deep dents all over make a
  model look crushed, not detailed. Lesson: detail the owner likes is *made* detail (bands, stitches, studs, wraps), with
  calm surfaces between them, like the mace.
- A second Claude started its own test page on port 8765, so Claude's page server moved to port 8771.
- The first try at the runes, wraps and studs was too thin to see, because they were smaller than the build grid
  (9 mm). Fixed by making them thicker and setting them further out from the cloth.

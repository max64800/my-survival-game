## 2026-11-22 — F601, F605, F606, F608, F609: Monster behaviour fixes

This session picked up five logged bugs and fixed them in one go.

### What we did

**F601 – Rock Snail spit fires through its own shell**
The snail plays a wind-up animation, then a timer calls `_spit_fx()` about 0.4 s later. If the snail tucked into its shell during that gap, the spit gob still launched. `monster_shell()` already removed the attack from the queue, but the timer kept running. Fix: at the top of `_spit_fx()` we now check the `shell_shut` flag — if the shell is closed, the function returns immediately without throwing the gob.

**F605 – Hollow Miner (medium skeleton) still doing standing stabs**
The lost_miner had three attacks: Frost Pick (jab), Pick Jab (second jab), and Leaping Chop (lunge). The owner wanted only the lunge. Removed Frost Pick and Pick Jab from `monsters.gd`. The skeleton now circles and waits when you're inside 2 m — that's intentional, it needs distance to leap.

**F606 – Bone Rot (small skeleton) had four attacks, two with no wind-up**
The tunnel_rat had Shovel Chop, Shovel Jab, and two faster attacks with tiny tell. Owner wanted only the overhand chop (readable wind-up). Left only the Shovel Chop in the attack list.

**F608 – Pit Boss chained two hits with no wind-up**
The Foreman's Great Chop had no cooldown (`rest` missing). After completing it he picked a new attack instantly — most of the time it was a second chop which had no visual tell from the player's perspective. Added `rest = 2.0` to Great Chop so there's a 2-second pause before he can swing again.

**F609 – Hollow Miner body-pushes the player when chasing**
Both the player and monsters use `MONSTER_LAYER` (bit 6) for physics collision. When the skeleton charges in CHASE state and runs into the player, their `move_and_slide()` calls create an overlap that the physics engine resolves by shoving the player backward. 

Tried adding a separate `MONSTER_PHASE_LAYER` to avoid changing dwarf.gd, but the simpler fix was behavioural: added a `no_push = true` data flag to `lost_miner`. In the CHASE state movement block, if `no_push` is set and the monster is already within 1.5 m of its target, it stops (`_move(delta, Vector3.ZERO, 0.0)`) instead of running into them. The skeleton still lunges correctly — the attack fires when it's at 2–3 m anyway.

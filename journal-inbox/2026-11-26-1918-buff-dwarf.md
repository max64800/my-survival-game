## Nov 26: the buff dwarf (F916)

**What we did:** the player dwarf is now buff instead of fat (target: `assets/concepts/dwarf/dwarf_buff_target.png`), because plate armour poked through his soft belly.
- Body (`tools/asset_forge/builds/dwarf_body.py`): the round belly and soft sides are gone. The chest is the widest part, there's a slight taper to the waist and the arms and legs are thicker. Big shoulder and neck muscles, plus back muscles in a V.
- Skeleton (`DwarfProportions`): shoulder joints 1.15 → 1.3, hips 1.2 → 1.3 (legs a little apart).
- Arm gap: the arms hang 8° out from the body, on top of every move (`DwarfProportions.set_arm_gap`, `DwarfRig.BODY_ARM_GAP`).
- Leather and copper armour rebuilt on the new body with `dwarf_armor.py`.
- New `glb` flag for the fast build: a grey, unpainted, weighted dwarf for shape pages.

**Rounds with the owner** (grey 3D page, old dwarf next to new, walking):
1. Belly gone, but boxy.
2. Wider chest. Owner: "even bigger shoulders/arms".
3. Bigger deltoids, arms and trapezius, with one broad chest plate instead of two round chest muscles. Owner: "still has woman boobs".
4. OK'd. The cause wasn't only the round muscles. Seen from the side, the chest bulged out and curved back in underneath. There were also lines under the chest and down its middle (they read as cleavage), and a round stomach lump made a ball low on the front. Fix: the front runs nearly straight from the collar to the waist, like a breastplate, with no lines and no lumps.

**Mistakes and hurdles:**
- The fast build quit before saving a glb, so I added the `glb` flag.
- I judged the chest with the shirt on, which hid the problem. Rendering the bare body (shirt and shorts removed in a small Blender script) showed it straight away. Next time, look at a body shape without clothes first.
- The arm gap broke the two-handed axe test: it pulled the two fists apart on the handle. Fix: the gap fades out when the arm is raised or reaching forward, and switches off while a two-handed weapon is held.
- The great-swing test is touchy. It passed alone and failed inside the suite, so I ran the old skeleton as a baseline to see what was really mine. With the fix it passes in the suite too.
- 4 test_dwarf failures (aim script error, stun meter, camera near a rock, hair tip in a dash) also fail with the old skeleton, so they aren't from this change (probably other chats' unfinished work).
- During the work I kept the skeleton change off disk (git checkout after each dump), so other chats' game runs didn't get a skeleton that didn't match the old body.
- `dwarf_rig.gd` and `FEEDBACK.md` had other chats' unsaved edits, so I committed only my own lines.

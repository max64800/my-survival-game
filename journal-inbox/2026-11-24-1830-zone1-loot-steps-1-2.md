# Zone 1 loot plan, steps 1 and 2: fixed dungeon recipes, copper pages in ruins (F844), Nov 24

**What we did:**
- **Step 1, each dungeon always teaches the same recipe.** Before, each world shuffled which dungeon of a size taught
  which copper recipe. Now every dungeon in `DungeonList.LIST` names its own recipe, in the owner's order (helm
  Mossgate, boots Lampwick, greaves Old Pell's, crossbow Mossprop, mail Hald, shield Shieldbearers, spear Barrows,
  axe Brassgate, sword Orrin, mace Emberdeep, great maul Hammerfall, great axe Cinderhold). Copper for now; bronze
  takes over in step 5. The shuffle (`DungeonLoot.recipes_for`) is gone. The four dungeon scrolls that disagreed
  (Mossgate, Lampwick, Pell, Mossprop) now teach the new recipe. Test `test_each_dungeon_teaches_its_own_recipe`
  also checks every recipe stand's scroll teaches its dungeon's recipe.
- **Step 2, copper recipes in ruins.** New table `Recipes.RUIN_COPPER` (which ruin teaches which copper armour/weapon).
  When a ruin is built, its main chest (the one with the most recipe pages) also gets its named page(s), e.g.
  "Armourer's Page: Copper Helm" in the Watchtower. Other chests keep the random food and tool pages. The Copper
  Pickaxe stays free. Tests: every copper armour/weapon is taught by exactly one ruin, each of those ruins is one the
  game builds and has a real main chest, and the Watchtower build test finds the Copper Helm page in a chest.

## Nov 27: the play-session list (F924, F927, F928, F929, F940, F417 looked at)

**What we did**
- **F924 (first perk point):** walking within 20 m of the first tower while still level 1 now gives exactly enough XP to reach level 2, so you get your first perk point there. The host checks this once a second.
- **F928 (read a page in a chest):** right-click a blueprint page inside a chest to learn it right there. You don't have to carry it out first. The host checks you're close enough to the chest.
- **F929 (ruin blueprints only by rebuilding):** the Watchtower and Outpost blueprints can no longer be found as pages. You learn them by rebuilding their ruin. The tower's roof chest now holds a normal blueprint page. Reading a "rebuild-only" page explains why it doesn't work yet.
- **F927 (aiming while looking up):** the camera now rises a little, slides right and moves in, so the dwarf stops blocking the crosshair. The owner picked "slide to the side".
- **F940 (the road):** the road between places is now packed sand: pale tan, with ripples and small pebbles. It digs like dirt and gives dirt. It is also "pulled tight like a string": from each stop it runs straight to the furthest stop it can reach. That only works through open air, with no column in the way and a floor that doesn't jump more than 2 m. Straight stretches went from 6.6 m to 14 m on average.
- **F417 (low FPS):** read three play logs. The worst moments (6-second freezes) come right after a big ruin is built all at once (Brickcrown: 1046 pieces; Minehead: 816 pieces plus digging). Draw calls were very high at those moments. The fix is a bigger job, so it goes to a new chat.

**Mistakes and hurdles**
- My feedback-moving helper rebuilds the lists from the last save. I ran it twice before saving, so the first item's line was lost. Fixed with a follow-up save. Lesson: save between runs.
- The helper also missed "F940 🔁 (F900)." because it looked for "F940." Fixed by matching "F940 " too.
- old_trail.gd ends its lines Windows-style (CRLF), so a plain text replace didn't match. Fixed by handling the line endings in the edit script.
- My first straight-road test failed. The new roads were only 10 m per straight piece, because a 1.2 m floor bump limit stopped most straight pieces. I counted why each straight piece failed, then loosened the limit to 2 m. The road is still dropped onto the real floor anyway, and big drops are still skipped.
- **Real bug found on the way:** after the road moved, crystals and roots grew on it. The game saves where plants and crystals go (PlanCache) and only re-plans when certain files change. The road file wasn't on that list, so the old plans (made for the old road) were reused. The road file is now on the list. That would have hit real players too.
- Some failing checks belong to other chats' unsaved work: test_dwarf, test_skeleton_miners, test_ruin_tower chest, test_pois columns, test_item_sources. I left them alone.

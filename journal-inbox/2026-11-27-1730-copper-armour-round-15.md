## 2026-11-27 · Copper armour round 15: shaped helmet face, arm plates back in (F914)

**What we did:** The owner said the new solid helmet face was "too plain, the entire thing blocked, so much dead space under the eyes", and that the upper-arm plates stuck out too far.
- **Helmet face:** it now has a shape like the picture's. A raised frame goes only round the eyes and stops just past them, so the temples step back. Under the eyes there's only a thin edge, and the nose guard narrows to a point. To make the point, the nose guard's corners are slid in toward the middle, so its edges stay clean.
- **Arm plates:** they sit 1.7 cm off the arm instead of 3.4 cm, and they're a bit smaller again.

**Mistakes and hurdles:**
- Claude's round-14 "solid face plate" (reaching down to 1.0 m) was the cause of the dead space. Claude had suggested it from a list, and it made the face blockier, the opposite of the picture.
- The first fix put the plate edge at 1.047 m, and his bare nose and cheeks showed under the eyes. At 1.035 m (where round 13 had it) the moustache starts right under the edge.

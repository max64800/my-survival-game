# F8 item menu tabs (F842), Nov 24

**What we did:** The F8 item menu was one huge list. Now it has tabs: All, Materials, Craftable, Farmable, Equipment, Food, Drinks, Building, Other.
Search looks only inside the open tab (All = everything). Code: `src/ui/dev_items.gd` (`tabs_of`, `all_ids(filter, tab)`).

**How items are sorted:** Each item has one main tab from its kind (food, drink, tools/weapons/armour/ammo = Equipment, stations/chests = Building,
ore/gems/fuel/runes = Materials, the rest = Other). Craftable (is the output of a recipe) and Farmable (seeds, plantable mushrooms, crops) overlap with those.

**Test:** `test_dev_item_menu_tabs` (every item in a tab, food/drink/equipment/seed in the right tab, search works per tab).

**Hurdle:** I tried a HUD photo with a tiny script run; Godot hung (script mode skips the game's autoloads) and I stopped it. No photo; the owner's play test is the look check.

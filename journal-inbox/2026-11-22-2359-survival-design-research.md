# Nov 22 – Second research round: survival game design lessons

**What we did**
- The owner picked 5 more research topics: Steam reviews of similar survival games, group fights + dwarf camera + sprint feel, what makes building fun, dark caves that still read well, and the settings players expect. (Steam page/wishlists was skipped; already done.)
- Five research helpers wrote notes in `docs/research/research_notes/Survival game design lessons/`; a writer turned them into `docs/research/reports/Survival game design lessons.md` with a 31-row ranked action table.
- Added the findings to the to-do list as F758-F779 and bumped F469 (camera too high) and F518 (can't see attacks from behind).

**Biggest lessons**
- Playing with friends is the top praise; broken joins are the top complaint.
- The cheapest wins are settings: key changes, subtitles, volume sliders, comfort switches.
- Fights: "attack tickets" so only 1-2 enemies hit one dwarf at once, plus edge arrows for off-screen hits.
- No source gave dwarf-sized camera numbers; every camera and sprint number is a guess to test in play.

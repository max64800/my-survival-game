## Nov 22 (PC, evening): the new build menu (F709)

**What we did**
- Rebuilt the B build menu from the mock-up the owner OK'd (`assets/concepts/ui/menu_build_v2_mockup.png`).
  It's now a wide bar along the bottom of the screen, so the green preview piece stays visible above it.
- Left, "1 Material": 4 big cards (Rootwood, Cut Stone, Clay Brick, Copper) with "you have N". If you have none, the
  card is faded and says where it's made. Greyed out if the chosen piece can't be made from it.
- Middle, "2 Piece": 6 big picture tabs instead of 13 (Floors, Walls, Roofs, Stairs, Home, Lights). Home has small
  buttons inside (Furniture, Decor, Living, Kitchen, Trophies). Piece cards get a green tick when you can build them
  right now; faded cards say on the card what's missing ("need 2 more Rootwood Plank" or "Needs Workbench level 2").
  A switch "Only show what I can build now" (off at the start). The cards scroll if there are more than 2 rows.
- Right: the chosen piece big, with each thing it needs as "have / need" + tick, the station line, and a gold
  "Left click in the world to place" (or "Still missing something" when you can't yet).
- Under it: the keys as little key caps.
- `hud.gd`: the menu now sits at the bottom centre (and moves along when the window changes size).
- The photo robot `menucheck` wasn't hooked up in `main.gd` any more, so it couldn't start; hooked it up again and
  gave it a 4th photo of the build menu.
- Tests: the tab test now checks every piece is on exactly one of the 6 tabs; new test that the 6 tabs exist and a
  piece you can afford gets the tick (and one you can't says what's missing).

**Mistakes and hurdles (and fixes)**
- My new test called `BuildMode.choose()`, which needs the whole game around it: set the piece and material directly.
- The first test game crashed twice while loading a dungeon model at the start (not the menu code; most likely
  another chat changing models at the same moment). The third try ran fine.
- The first robot run took no photos: `menucheck` wasn't in `main.gd`'s list of robots. Added it.
- First screenshot: the key caps and the "1"/"2" badges were unreadable, because the kit's ornate gold frame
  pictures swallow tiny text. Drew them as plain rounded boxes instead. The "Only show..." switch had no visible
  box (the game's theme has no checkbox picture): made it a toggle button that glows gold, with "[x]" when on.
  The Home tab had no picture, and the red notes on faded cards were cut off: picked the first Home piece that has
  a picture, made cards a bit taller and shortened the station note on the card (the right box shows it whole).
- Noticed: the Furniture pieces (beds etc.) have no menu pictures at all (also true in the old menu). Asked the owner.

My game finally has a name.

## 057 · Digging
HOOKS
1. ★ Digging in my game felt like hitting a pillow. (mistake, funny)
2. ★ In a dwarf game, digging has to feel amazing. (stakes)
3. Conan Exiles gets one thing perfect: hitting rock.
4. Watch the cracks spread.
5. Every hit now pays you.
6. A pickaxe takes 10 hits. An axe takes 2.
7. ★ I made rocks break into real pieces. (visual)
8. Digging in my game: before and after.
9. Which game has the best digging?
10. My rocks used to just vanish.

SCRIPT
```
Digging in my game felt like hitting a pillow.
You swing, and the rock just vanishes. Nothing comes back.
In a dwarf game, that's the worst mistake I could make.
So I studied my favourite: Conan Exiles. Hitting rock there feels great.
Now every hit pays you a stone or some ore.
Cracks spread across the rock with every swing.
Then it shatters into real chunks.
And the right tool matters. A stone axe chops a root in 2 hits. A pickaxe needs about 10.
Wishlist Dwarves Never Fall on Steam.
```

## 059 · 34 seconds
HOOKS
1. ★ My game froze for 34 seconds every time it loaded. (number + pain)
2. ★ 34 seconds to 10. One change. (before/after)
3. The problem was pebbles.
4. Tiny rocks almost broke my game.
5. I put a stopwatch on every part of my game.
6. ★ Pebbles took 26 of those 34 seconds. (surprise)
7. Game freezing? Time every step.
8. What's the longest loading screen you've sat through?
9. My caves used to take 92 seconds to load.
10. The smallest thing in my game was the slowest.

SCRIPT
```
My game froze for 34 seconds every time it loaded.
Just a stuck screen, and no idea why.
So Claude put a stopwatch on every step of loading.
The answer was tiny: pebbles.
Pebbles and dripstone took 26 of those 34 seconds.
Thousands of them, all planned at once, while the whole game waited.
Now a helper plans them in the background.
Loading went from 34 seconds to 10.
Wishlist Dwarves Never Fall on Steam.
```

## 061 · Small things
HOOKS
1. ★ My spear swung like a sword. (mistake, gamers notice)
2. ★ My crafting table was almost as tall as my dwarf. (funny, visual)
3. Players notice the tiny things.
4. You could build a table inside a wall.
5. Three small mistakes. Three fixes.
6. ★ Spears should stab. Mine didn't. (gamer logic)
7. Would you notice this?
8. Bad survival games skip these details.
9. My dwarf couldn't reach the table.
10. What tiny thing ruins a survival game for you?

SCRIPT
```
My spear swung like a sword.
Spears stab. Every survival game player knows that.
Now it points forward, and you thrust it straight.
Mistake two: my crafting table was almost as tall as my dwarf.
I made it 20% smaller. Now he can actually use it.
Mistake three: you could build a table halfway inside a wall.
Now it turns red, and the game won't let you place it.
Small things, but players notice.
Wishlist Dwarves Never Fall on Steam.
```

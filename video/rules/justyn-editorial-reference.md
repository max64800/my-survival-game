On 2026-10-16 Max sent https://www.tt.site/t/ZSgFL1hH8/ (@justyn.ai, 53 s, 77K views): "The way he edited this video was really good. I really loved it. So take a lot of inspiration from it for the future videos."

Full breakdown: work/research-2026-10-12/notes/6-1-justyn.ai.md. The video and contact sheets are in videos/6-1 and frames/6-1.

The core to reuse:
- **One design system:** cream paper cards, forest green #1F4032, gold #B08A3E, burgundy #6B2027; editorial condensed serif; mono caps kickers; a script accent word.
- **Giant serif numbers/words behind his head** for each point and the last line.
- **UI props that act out the verb:** effort dial turns to MAX, red pen strikes a requirement, calendar fills then empties to "free", envelope opens, render bar fills, comment box types the CTA keyword.
- **One full-width kinetic punchline card** per video; colour-bar wipes as transitions.
- **Cards are small, in the top third, and never on the face.** They stay up only while their sentence plays.
- **Multiple camera angles** (close, wide seated, wide standing) cutting on sentence changes.

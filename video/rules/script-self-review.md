Max (2026-10-16): when I create a script, I must look at it again, go back through all the rules, analyse the script against every one of them, find ways to improve it, and then give him the improved version. Starts with the next script.

**Why:** scripts kept needing rounds of his feedback (e.g. the lazy-superpower v2→v3: a line that didn't make sense, a weak payoff). He wants the checking done before it reaches him.

**How to apply (every script or rewrite, before showing it):**
1. Write draft 1 (don't show it yet).
2. Audit it against every rule source, line by line:
   - [[talking-head-rulebook]] (`video-scripts/talking-head-rulebook.md`, all 55 rules);
   - [[jenny-hoyos-formula]]: problem + "so I'm going to…" + win condition in 3 s, foreshadowing, end right after the payoff;
   - 25-35 s spoken (~70-90 words), payoff in sentence 2, no backstory at 3-7 s (the measured YouTube drop is at 4-6 s);
   - every line gives value or forward pull; cut any line that doesn't;
   - [[say-full-place-in-dubai]] when a place is named; [[content-direction]] for the topic (not real estate by default);
   - an ending with punch (not a vague "follow for more"), plus a comment-keyword CTA where it fits;
   - easy to say out loud: short sentences, no tongue-twisters, his natural words;
   - lines that give the edit something to show (a number, a UI moment, a before/after); see [[justyn-editorial-reference]];
   - the 5-point audience check (added 2026-10-25, [[reference-batch-10-2026-10-25]]): talks to ONE person, speaks to their current situation, gives ONE transformation (not random tips), uses their words (no jargon), ends with a clear next step.
3. Rewrite to fix every miss, then re-check the new version.
4. Show Max: the FINAL script, a short audit table (rule → pass, or what changed and why), and the 10 hooks with the top 3 marked ([[ten-hooks-every-script]]).

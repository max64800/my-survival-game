**The formula (3 parts)**
1. **Set expectations in the first ~3 s: state the PROBLEM + what I'm GOING TO DO about it.** Shape: "[situation]. But I don't want to [cost/pain]. 2. **Foreshadow → FOMO**: promise a specific reveal the viewer must stay for ("prove her wrong with the best present for $5" → you stay to see the gift).
3. **End RIGHT after the payoff**: no recap, no outro, no logo, no CTA tail — viewers swipe right after the payoff, so the video ends there (they've already watched 100%, and it loops).
Also "every second counts": no setup sentences, every line moves the challenge forward.

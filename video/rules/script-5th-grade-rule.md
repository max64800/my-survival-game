Every script (Shorts and long-form) must read at a 5th grade level: short sentences, everyday words, explain any technical word in plain words the first time. Measure the Flesch-Kincaid grade of the spoken lines and rewrite until it is about 5 or lower.

**Why:** Max, 26 Oct 2026: "5th grader level text so very easy to understand ... that has to be a rule". Global audience, many non-native English speakers.

**How to apply:** add the grade score to the script audit table (see [[script-self-review]], [[talking-head-rulebook]]).

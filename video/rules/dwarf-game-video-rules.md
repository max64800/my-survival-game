Max (2026-11-17) on the dwarf game series (049-055 and later):
1. It's fine to say "I'm building it with AI", but AI is NOT the hook or the main topic. The hook and topic are the game, the journey and the mistakes. Mention Claude/AI in one line, in the middle.
   (His words: "I am okay saying I am building it with AI but it should be the hook or the main topic". Read as "should NOT be"; confirm if a script shows otherwise.)
2. Show the journey and ALL his mistakes: the before/after, what went wrong, what he changed.
3. Survival games are his favourite genre and he is passionate about them. Big inspirations: Valheim, Grounded, 7 Days to Die, Conan Exiles. OK to name them as inspirations (the game itself must never copy them).
4. His earlier, first game is Deep Rise (on Steam, launched Nov 13, 2026, a week late). It can appear in some videos, not every one.

**Why:** he wants a game-dev journey channel feel, not another "AI built X" video.
**How to apply:** every dwarf-game script: hook = a game moment, mistake or passion; a mistake + fix in every video; AI at most one line; Deep Rise in about 1 in 4 videos. Related: [[content-direction]], [[jenny-hoyos-formula]].

**Kelssie (@kelssie3, real-estate agent, Max: "the best captions")**: two layers, both BEHIND her (matted): (1) quiet one-word reader caption, plain white, under the chin (Poppins 600 ~105 px or Inter Light), hard cuts; (2) every 1-3 s a 2-4 line headline lockup (thin connector + heavy word + italic serif accent in ONE accent colour per video, tight negative leading, zig-zag), lines blur-rise ~0.17-0.2 s on their word, stack drifts up, then defocuses/smears out; reader caption hides while a lockup shows. Extras: circle→pill chips for 3-item lists, red strike for myths, card/logo over an open palm, iMessage CTA. Her produced edits get 58-104K views vs 14.9K for a plain selfie. Built as caption style c1-02 (+ twist c1-03).

**Audio advice to apply**
- Voice: -14 to -16 LUFS integrated, LRA < 5 (Kelssie's voice is heavily compressed, even). Talking pieces: no music under voice (music only under b-roll/tours).
- Cut the music ~1-1.2 s before the payoff/logo, bring it back on the reveal (rikibosso). Riser at the start, sub-bass booms on only 2-3 big reveals. Very faint sparkle ticks (< -35 dB) on pill arrivals.
- Music beds from short mood loops crossfaded (0.5-1 s) on story beats instead of one track (multiply.sound).

**Caption/format advice**
- Big caption level = summarised claim, small level = verbatim (brooke_wrightmode) → fits the "levels" captions.
- Active-word weight pulse: serif word enters semibold, relaxes to regular ~0.35 s later (karan).
- Persistent comment-keyword CTA pill ("comment PALM") for the whole CTA; "save this"; follow promise with a clear niche.
- Hooks: specific outcome + time frame + "here's what I'd do"; money-anchor hooks; three negations → reveal ("not X, not Y, it's Z"); show the finished result first, then the how.
- White exposure flash on A-roll angle changes is common, but ASK Max first (he bans flashes between b-roll).
- Brand/portal logos (Property Finder, Bayut) appear in her edits — check usage rights before using any on Max's videos.

**Timing/edit advice (round 2)**
- Fire visual accents ~2 frames BEFORE the word/beat (the eye needs a moment); accent ~1 in 3 candidate words, never evenly spaced; put the biggest contrast change on the payoff.
- Music bed 10-12 dB under the voice. Keep a core SFX set of 10 roles (counting, glitch, whoosh, metallic riser, low hit, flash, flash+shutter, page flip, shine, pop).
- Grade: drop highlights, lift shadows slightly, saturation -10% then warm tones back, light grain, same grade on every clip.
- Ideas to try (~20% change): object between his hands, glass notification stack for list beats, gold italic display serif (Playfair/DM Serif) for emotional words, split layout (graphic top 55-60%, Max in a card below), fixed-playhead milestone strip, two-step share→save ending.

Its "AI slop" list (counters, typed headlines, status-dot cards) conflicts with Max's scores — HIS scores win (he rated counters/UI cards 8).

- Advice: rotate proven formats (one idea → 5 posts in different formats, let numbers decide); stack voice + text + visual hooks in the first 2 s; film ~20 b-roll clips in one session; post, then refine with data; profile ladder (clear face photo → first name + niche → who you help → proof → CTA with DM keyword); comment-keyword lead magnets; Claude tools worth building: flop-video analyzer, comment miner, competitor analyzer.

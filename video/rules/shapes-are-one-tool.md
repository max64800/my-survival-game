Max on culture v4 (IMG_3873, 2026-10-10). He rated it 6-7/10 and said to post it as is: no more renders of it.

- **Keep explaining ideas through shapes.** He likes it (the spiky circle infecting the others, the balloon popping, the gold dot hopping across blue circles, the 150-dot counter).
- **But shapes are one tool, not the whole video.** In future edits, mix them with other visuals: emojis, flat clip-art and stickers, UI cards, kit4 morphs. Pick the best tool for each line. Don't run one technique start to finish.
- **A backing plate IS needed behind graphics.** No box looked busy over his hair and forehead, since there's no wall above his head in close-ups. A full-width card was too big, with dead space. So: a fitted plate sized to what's shown, which resizes and morphs between beats:
  - square for a single cluster ("BAD ATTITUDE");
  - wide for a row (3 companies, the 150-dot grid);
  - enough padding at the bottom so labels and counters never touch the edge (the 150-agents plate was too tight at the bottom).
- **Transitions between shapes should be smoother, "like water"**, running into the next formation. Use liquid/gooey morphs: an SVG goo filter (blur + alpha threshold, final result crisp), elastic easing, staggered flow along curved paths, droplets stretching into the next shape. No straight linear moves.

**Why:** he wants variety and premium, liquid motion, not a single repeated gimmick.

**How to apply:** when planning a video's visuals, choose a tool per beat. When shapes are used, wrap them in a content-fitted plate that animates its size between beats, and connect formations with liquid morphs.

Max asked (2026-10-06): give 10 hooks with every script I write, including rewrites and small revisions. Mark the top 3 with a one-line reason; he picks.

**Why:** He likes choosing the hook himself, and the best hook is rarely the first one.

**How to apply:** Built into the eqt-script-doctor skill (.claude/skills/eqt-script-doctor). Never hand over a script without the 10-hook list. Related: [[editing-taste-profile]].

**Hook ladder (added 2026-10-25, from @kienobimedia in [[reference-batch-10-2026-10-25]]):** spread the 10 hooks across these types and label each one: plain value → + timeframe → flip a belief → "I did the research" → "most think X, it's not even Y, it's Z" → story question. Same topic, a bigger curiosity gap each step. Also offer one 2nd-person identity hook ("you're about to become…", the 808K pattern) where it fits.

`video-scripts/talking-head-rulebook.md` holds the research Max had Claude do on 2026-10-14. Master rule: every second must deliver value or make the viewer need the next second.

Main pieces:
- a pre-record checklist
- 55 rules across hooks, structure, story, language, delivery, persuasion and visuals
- platform metrics: a cliff at 0-3 s = hook, a slide in the middle = late payoff, a drop at the end = weak close
- a fill-in template: HOOK <10 words / PAYOFF sentence 2 / LOOP / BUT / OBJECTION / ACTION / CLOSE / CTA

The eqt-script-doctor skill now reads it and adds a RULEBOOK CHECK to each script.

Tension with [[edit-hard-rules]]: rule 35 says keep a short pause before the payoff, while the edit rules say remove breaths. Keep ~0.3-0.4 s before the payoff line only. Related: [[jenny-hoyos-formula]], [[ten-hooks-every-script]].

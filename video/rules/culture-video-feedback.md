Max on the culture video v1 (2026-10-10): "probably the worst one we've done". What went wrong, now RULES:

1. **Captions must be readable first.**
   - Big (at least the v1 chunk size: Inter about 62-70 px plus a larger key word).
   - Highlighted key word (marker / serif / tag).
   - Words close together, never spread across the frame.
   - On screen long enough to read: never vanish fast.
   - NEVER behind his head and NEVER behind or under a graphic.
   - The karan-style serif/script stack (kit5) failed on these counts. Don't use it again unless he asks, and if so, much bigger and slower.
2. **Graphics must be big and fully visible.**
   - Nothing cut off (emoji cells were clipped).
   - Labels at least about 36 px ("COMPANY CULTURE" was too small to see).
3. **No background swap / green-screen effect.** The cut-out quality looks bad. Don't use it.
4. **Nothing behind his head that he covers.** If he fills most of the frame, don't put text behind him ("BOUTIQUE" was unreadable).
5. **Variety inside a video.** The 1-19 s stretch was "the same box with emojis". Every beat needs a visibly different idea.
6. **Retire the grey frosted box + emoji + text formula.** After 3 videos it reads as "basic AI slop". Keep the morph/flow idea, but show what he's saying through **animated shapes and metaphors**. His example:
   - a group of circles;
   - one becomes spiky;
   - wherever its spikes touch, the others slowly turn green → red;
   - the spiky one gets redder and redder.
   Brainstorm visual metaphors per line (shape-language motion design, like @motionlovee / @thefusionlabfx), not UI cards with emojis.
7. **Kept and liked:** the ending (the EQT logo reveal + EQT.ae).

Max on culture v2 (2026-10-10), "better but looks cheap". More rules:
- **No big coloured half-screen panel** (the navy split looked odd).
- **No glowing or blurry shapes**, no drop-shadow glows, no big glowing balloon or yellow block: "looks like PowerPoint / cheap AI". Shapes must be crisp, flat and smooth, Apple-like (iOS system colours). (v3 put them in a frosted card; superseded by the v3 note below: no box.)
- **Liked:** the shape storytelling idea (the spiky circle infecting the others, 0.8-13 s) and the 150-dots counter (24-26 s). The idea of gold client dots was good; only the execution was too blurry.
- **Story logic must make sense:** after someone is removed, don't show the effect continuing unless the script brings them back ("otherwise…" means show him coming back, then the poison).
- **Layering:** small elements must never slide behind other shapes (a gold dot went behind a blue one).
- **End reveal:** the face bubble must not cover "REAL ESTATE". Put it lower.
- **Captions:** the font and highlights (marker / tag / colour / caps) are good, but one line is "very basic". He wants LEVELS: line 1 appears, then line 2 below, then line 3, with older lines still on screen, then the whole block clears.

Max on culture v3 (2026-10-10): "a lot cleaner and a lot better", the crisp no-blur shapes and level captions worked. Loves the little gold dot hopping across the blue circles. But:
- **No container box around the motion graphics.** The full-width dark card had too much dead space left and right. Let crisp shapes float on the video by themselves (thin dark text shadow on labels for contrast).
- **Use a small plate only where tiny elements would vanish** on the background, like the 150 small grey agent dots. It appears for that beat and then goes away.
- Labels never overlap shapes. Put headings below the cluster when the top is near the 250 px safe line.
- A reveal circle must open fast, never sit as a slow dark dot on his face.

Culture v4 (no box) was rated 6-7/10 and posted as is. He reversed the no-box idea: a plate IS needed, but fitted to the content. See [[shapes-are-one-tool]].

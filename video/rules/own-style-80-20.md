- animations that don't flow into each other (separate cards popping in and out instead of one morphing plate);
- the colour-bar wipe transitions;
- captions repeating the same text as the graphic at the top;

- **Don't:** use a cream panel, colour-bar wipes, or plate text that repeats the caption word for word.

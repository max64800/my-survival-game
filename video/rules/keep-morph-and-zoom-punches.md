Max, 2026-10-08, after the coffee video (IMG_3822):
- "The transitions between the motion graphics is amazing! We should keep this for every video." So every animated edit uses a morphing graphic chain: one card or element that changes shape and content beat to beat (kit4 `Morph`, see [[motion-flow-rules]]), never separate graphics popping in and out.
- "I love the zoom in and out during my sentences, keep those as a rule. It was great especially at the last sentence." So every edit gets camera punches inside sentences: zoom in (1.12-1.22, fast 0.18 s) on key words, ease back out (1.04, 0.32 s) at the next phrase, with gentle reframes between. The last sentence (24.5 → 25.96 "sad" 1.12 → 26.9 "happy" 1.2 + impact) is the reference.

Counter rule from the same video: odometer counters must sit on clean numbers at rest. Start a little above the target (32 → 10) and roll fast, landing exactly on the spoken word.

**How to apply:** plan the morph states and the punch-ins first for every new video. Vary the look (colours, card styles) between videos, but keep these two mechanics.

More notes from the "why I quit" video (IMG_3828, 2026-10-09):
- **No thick red tilted strike lines** ("don't look slick, looks like paint"). Strike-throughs are a thin straight soft-white line (3 px, slight glow) while the word dims to ~45% and shrinks ~5% (kit4 now does this).
- **A morph state must never sit empty** while he talks. Show what he's saying right away (e.g. "🏠 buy or sell in Dubai?"), then morph to the next state on its word.
- **Check every cut point for silence**, especially before a new sentence ("…a little. [pause] I'm an introvert" had 0.5 s of dead air).

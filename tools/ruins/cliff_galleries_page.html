Cliff Galleries

Ruin 13, buildings climbing up a tall cave wall. Every building is stone at the bottom and brick on top. You climb it like a mountain: from the hall at the foot, over a bridge, up the cliff, along a hanging gallery, to a watch tower high on a rock shelf. Press "The whole blueprint" to see it as it was built.

The parts

  • Stone Hall10 × 12 m at the cliff's foot, its back half inside the rock. A railed terrace and a wide stair in front, a tall stone hall (two walls high) with a round door, stairs up to its roof terrace (battlements). On the roof a brick storey with a steep roof that juts out 2 m on both sides on stone brackets
  • Bridgea railed brick bridge over the crack, on braced brick posts 10 m tall, from the hall's brick storey to the Ledge House
  • Ledge Houseon the right rock ledge, 4.5 m up. A stone storey (its back in the rock), a brick storey that juts out over brackets, a steep roof with 2 rows each side
  • Cliff stairsfrom the Ledge House's upper door: a brick landing on a stone pier, then two flights up the cliff face to the gallery
  • Hanging Gallerya railed brick walk along the cliff 11.75 m up, on brick posts standing on the hall's roof, to the left shelf
  • Watch Toweron the left shelf, 10.75 m up: two stone storeys, a brick storey jutting out front and left on brackets, a railed deck on top and the brick lookout with a steep 4-sided roof (22.5 m: the highest point). The best chest goes up here
  • Store shedat the shelf's foot: stone floor, brick walls, a lean-to roof against the rock, steps to its door

7 flights of stairs, 9 doors. Every stair, door, railing and bracket is checked by the game's tests. Made only from pieces the game has. The shelf, the ledge and the crack are the cave's own rock, not built. The cave needs to be about 29 m tall here.

    Your call

    Does this feel like a grand ruin worth climbing? Look from the front, then "Hall and bridge", "Ledge House" and "The tower".

    Changed from the shape model: the lookout is square with a steep 4-sided roof (an 8-sided brick lookout can't be built from real pieces, and the pointed spire is Brickcrown's), the jutting storeys stick out 2 m (the game's smallest floor), and the timber hoist arm is left out for now.

    In the ruin view the gallery's middle has fallen onto the hall's roof, the bridge's far end sags, the tower's roof leans, the roofs have holes and some brick has fallen. In the game the chests, junk and creatures come next.

Hearth Manor

The seventh ruin, and the second grand one: the home of a rich Hearthbrick family. A two-floor hall on a raised terrace with a wide stair up to its round front door, a wing on the left with a balcony on brackets, a low passage on the right to a big three-floor tower with a lookout room on its roof and a walk-around gallery. Left: rebuilt with root planks. Middle: rebuilt with clay bricks, as it first stood. Right: the ruin as you find it.

What you find

  • Hall10 × 8 m, 2 floors, steep roof with 3 roof windows
  • Wing (left)8 × 4 m, 2 floors, balcony on 9 brackets
  • Tower (right)8 × 8 m, 3 floors + lookout, 11.75 m tall
  • Terrace in front10 × 4 m, 3 flights of steps
  • Whole placeabout 31 × 16 m

The tower's pointed roof blew off, and the back-right corner of its lookout fell with a piece of the gallery (the rubble lies beside the tower). The hall's roof caved in at its back-left corner and took a piece of the upstairs floor with it. Half of the wing's balcony fell: its brackets still stick out of the wall. One terrace railing is down, the doors are gone, some walls are worn. The family's things are still there: the game table and high chair by the sideboard, the bedroom upstairs (bed, wardrobe, desk, books, wash stand), the bread oven in the wing, scrolls and books up the tower (12 pieces from the home decor sets). Three chests (one up in the lookout) and four rock beetles (left off this page to keep it small).

Rebuild in wood

    What fell comes back in wood; the brick that still stands stays (whole and worn walls too, unless something new goes on top of one). The brackets, trim bands and roof windows are Hearthbrick pieces, so in wood they are left off.

    Rebuild in brick

      Worn walls are rebuilt too, so every gap closes. Either way the wreckage is cleared and you learn both blueprints, the Wooden Manor and the Hearth Manor, plus building XP. Building the whole Hearth Manor from nothing costs 3,195 clay bricks, 434 mortar and 18 copper ingots (the Wooden Manor: 4,449 root planks, 351 rope, 229 clay bricks, 17 mortar).

      What makes it different

      Built the grand-ruin way: four parts at four heights (the low passage, the wing, the hall, the tower), a roofline that changes every few metres (three roof windows on the hall, one on the wing, the tower's pointed roof), the wing's balcony on brackets and the tower's roof gallery. Every walkway is a whole floor tile, so a dwarf can walk everywhere. A trim band runs along every floor line, with corner blocks.

      The eye lands on the round front door: the wide stair climbs the terrace straight to it, under a wide window. Inside, stairs go up in the hall and in the wing (each lands on a 2 m floor), and the tower has three spiral stairs up to the lookout, each going in and coming out onto open floor. The wing's upstairs door opens onto the balcony; the lookout's door opens onto the gallery. Round doors only, no bracket over a door.

      Uses 4 of your 5 new Hearthbrick pieces: brackets, trim band, trim corner and roof window (the narrow railing was only for the small balconies, which are gone). The furniture is only in the ruin: the rebuilt ones stay empty for you to furnish.

      "Mark new pieces" glows orange on every piece the rebuild adds.

Brickcrown Keep

Ruin 11, an old Stonehold stone fort that dwarves later built higher in Hearthbrick brick. Grey stone at the bottom with flat tops and battlements; warm brick on top with a sloped tile roof; a stone band at every floor line ties the two together. Its back is pushed into the cave wall. Press "The whole blueprint" to see it as it was built.

The parts

  • Keep14 × 12 m. Two tall stone storeys (the first 4.5 m, with a beam band), two buttresses and a round front door. Then a brick 3rd storey that juts out 1 m at the front on stone brackets, and a smaller brick 4th storey stepped back behind a railed terrace, with a tile roof, two dormers and a brick chimney (about 18 m up)
  • Left towerround (12 sides), joined to the keep by a door; stone to a railed deck with battlements at 7.5 m, then a thinner 6-sided brick top, 3 storeys with a ladder, and a railed lookout at 14.5 m
  • Curtain wallstone, 2 m thick, from the keep to the right tower: a round gateway with two gate doors in the middle, a passage inside, a walk on top with battlements on both sides. One crack mended in brick
  • Bridgea stone bridge on 3 carved columns across a dip in the cave floor, up to the gate
  • Right towerall stone, round, on a 2 m lump of rock: 4 storeys and a flat top with battlements, about 15 m up. A short wall runs from it to the cave rock
  • Courtyardbehind the gate, carved back into the cave rock: stone paving and a well, with 2 small stone rooms in the rock on its left (round doors, windows, a battlement top)

11 flights of stairs and 3 ladders, each starts and ends on whole floor. Made only from pieces the game already has, stone and brick mixed. The dip, the rock lump and the courtyard's hollow are made by the world, not by building pieces; in the game the hollow gets a lumpy, natural edge. The cave needs to be about 21 m tall here.

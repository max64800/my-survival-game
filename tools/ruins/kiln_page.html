The Old Kiln

The fifth ruin: where the kiln-master fired Hearthbrick. A fat round brick kiln under a ring of tile roof with a smoke hole in the middle, a lean-to shed against it, and the kiln-master's two-floor house with a walled yard. Left: rebuilt with root planks. Middle: rebuilt with clay bricks, as it first stood. Right: the ruin as you find it.

What you find

A piece of the kiln's upper wall broke off on its right side and fell across the shed, crushing its roof; it lies on the floor past the shed. The kiln's tile roof fell in on that side (two of its railings still stand), and a piece of the gallery floor fell in. Its last load of bricks still stands, half toppled, on the firing floor. The house's roof caved in at its left end and took the upstairs corner with it; doors gone, worn walls, two yard walls fallen. The kiln-master's things are still there: his kitchen downstairs, bed, wardrobe and desk upstairs, pot shelves in the kiln, charcoal in the shed, a clay bin and a tipped ash cart in the yard (21 pieces from the decor set). Three chests (one up on the kiln's gallery) and four rock beetles.

Rebuild in wood

    The house, the shed and the yard walls come back in wood; whole and worn brick walls stay (unless something new goes on top of one). The kiln itself is rebuilt in brick even here: a wooden oven would burn (like the Brewhall's chimney).

    Rebuild in brick

      Worn walls are rebuilt too, so every gap closes. Either way the wreckage is cleared and you learn both blueprints, the Wooden Kiln and the Hearthbrick Kiln, plus building XP.

      What makes it different

      The Kiln Crown (your idea): a ring of sloped tile roof all round the top, rising to a stone lip round a six-sided smoke hole, railings standing on the lip. The hole sits right over the gallery inside, whose middle is open too, so from the gallery you look down onto the firing floor and up through the roof. Stairs up on the back left, their foot on open floor.

      Two round doors into the kiln: the front one, and one on the right into the firewood shed. The house has a front door to the yard and a side door toward the kiln, steps landing on open ground, no window beside a door.

      New models: the Kiln Crown (a new building piece; suggested price 60 clay bricks, 12 mortar, 10 root planks), its broken version, the fallen wall chunk and a toppled brick stack (you hit it to clear it). The furniture is only in the ruin: the rebuilt ones stay empty for you to furnish.

      "Mark new pieces" glows orange on every piece the rebuild adds.

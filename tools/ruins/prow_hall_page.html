Prow Hall

Ruin 9, a Stonehold castle set into the cave. A great hall with a round tower on each corner, a pointed prow on the front like a ship's bow, a big round keep at the back sunk into the rock wall, and a bridge out to a lone tower on a rock lump. Press "Rebuilt in stone" to see the blueprint whole, or "Mended in wood" to see the ruin patched up with wood where it fell.

The parts

  • Great hall20 × 12 m, 2 storeys + roof walk
  • Prow8 m wide, a point of triangles, 3 storeys, the two main doors
  • 4 corner towersround (12 sides), front 4 storeys, back 5
  • Keepbig round tower, 15 m across, a small one on its roof, a crown of 6 balconies on brackets, about 15 m up
  • Bridge10 m long, 10 m up, over a dip in the cave floor
  • Lone toweron a 7 m rock lump, a fire bowl on top

Every part is a different height, stepping up to the keep. 29 flights of stairs, each starts and ends on whole floor. The cave shape (rock wall, lump, dip) is made by the world, not by building pieces.

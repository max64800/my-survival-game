Pillar Spiral

Ruin 15, a little town wound round a natural rock column. Stone at the bottom, brick in the middle, wood on top. You go in through the big round door, up through the Ring Hall onto its roof, then climb a wooden stair round and round the column, from house to house, up to the lookout at the top. In the ruin one flight of that stair has fallen, so you go up inside the Bunk House instead. Press "The whole blueprint" to see it as it was built.

The parts

  • The columna natural rock column through the middle, 10 m across at its foot, 7.6 m higher up, widening into the cave ceiling 25 m up. The cave's own rock, not built
  • Ring Hallstone, 12-sided, 22 m across, one tall storey round the column's foot. A big round main door at the front with a lantern each side, stairs up inside. Its flat roof is the terrace, with battlements
  • Plinth and grand stairstone, in front of the main door, steps down to the lower floor
  • The Spirala wooden stair round the column, one flight on each side, each a storey higher, on brick brackets
  • Lamp House (right)two brick storeys, a tile roof
  • Store House (back)two brick storeys, a wooden one jutting out on brackets, a back door onto the Shrine Bridge
  • Bunk House (left)three brick storeys, a wooden one jutting out on brackets
  • Bell Tower (front)four brick storeys right over the main door, a wider wooden one, a railed deck
  • Eyriea 6-sided wooden lookout on the deck under a pointed tiled spire: the highest point. The best chest goes up here
  • Arch Bridge and Kiln Housea stone bridge on carved columns over a crack, to a brick house with two chimneys on a rock lump
  • Shrine Bridge and Rock Shrinea short wooden bridge to a stone shrine on a high rock shelf, flat roof with battlements

Every door is round, no ladders or ramps. Every stair, door, railing and bracket is checked by the game's tests. Made only from pieces the game has.

    Changed from the shape model: the Eyrie is 6-sided, not 8 (the game's round shapes come in 6 and 12 sides), its spire is brick tiles (the spire piece only comes in brick), and there is no bell yet (the game has no bell piece).

    In the ruin view the left flight of the stair has fallen, the spire leans, a kiln chimney is broken, the roofs have holes and ceiling rubble lies on the terrace. In the game the chests, junk and creatures come next.

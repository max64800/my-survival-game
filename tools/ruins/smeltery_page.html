Smeltery Terraces

Ruin 10, an old dwarf bronze-works in Hearthbrick, built into the cave in three steps: a long row of brick vaults at the bottom, a smelting hall on their roof, and a tall chimney tower at the back. On the left a round furnace house, joined by a bridge over a dip in the floor. Press "Rebuilt in brick" to see the blueprint whole, or "Mended in wood" to see the ruin patched up with wood where it fell.

The parts

  • Vaultsa 30 m long row of brick store rooms, 3 m tall; their flat roof is the terrace you walk on
  • Yard + grand staira railed yard in front, a wide stair up to the terrace and the hall's main door
  • Smelting hall12 × 8 m on the terrace, a tall first floor (4.5 m, with a brick beam band), an upper floor that sticks out on brackets, a steep roof with two dormers, top about 14 m up
  • Chimney towersquare, 6 storeys, then 12-sided for a round room, then a crown of 5 balconies on brackets, about 18 m up
  • Furnace houseround (12 sides), 3 storeys and a railed roof deck, lower than the hall
  • Bridge8 m long, 3 m up, from the furnace house to the terrace, over a dip in the cave floor

Three steps up: vaults, hall, tower. Every part is a different height. 15 flights of stairs, each starts and ends on whole floor. The back is pushed into the cave wall; the dip under the bridge is made by the world, not by building pieces. The cave needs to be about 20 m tall here.

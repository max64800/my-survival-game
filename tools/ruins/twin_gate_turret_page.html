The Turret and the Short Battlement

Both are brand-new pieces, and each one is a single piece. They aren't put together from the building pieces you already have. In the 3D view they glow blue; everything around them is pieces you already know.

The walk-in turret

  • Size3.3 × 3.3 m, 3 m tall
  • Floor1.75 m up, level with the roof
  • Used6 tower corners

What's inside the one piece, from the bottom up (drag "Build it up" to watch it grow):

  1. 6 rings of stone, each wider than the one below, like an upside-down staircase. They carry the turret out past the tower's corner.
  2. A flagstone floor, at the same height as the tower roof, so you walk straight in.
  3. 2 rows of wall stones, about waist high.
  4. A copper band with rivets.
  5. A top row of stones and 7 stone teeth (merlons), the same shape as on the battlements.

Its back quarter is open: that part is the tower's own roof corner. Its side walls run along the roof's edges and meet the normal battlements there.

The short battlement

  • Size1 m long, 0.9 m tall
  • Used4, ends of the barracks roofs

The normal battlement cut in half: same stones and copper band, but 1 m long with one stone tooth in the middle instead of three. The barracks roofs stuck out 1 m past their battlements, and no 1 m piece existed, so this closes the gap. In the view it stands next to a normal 2 m battlement so you can compare.

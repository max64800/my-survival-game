The Lantern Spire

The fourth ruin: a tall, thin Stonehold beacon tower, a landmark to find. Left: rebuilt with root planks. Middle: rebuilt with cut stone, as it first stood. Right: the ruin as you find it, its top lying across the cave floor.

What you find

  • Round tower, 12 sides7.7 m wide
  • Floors (when whole)3 + lantern gallery
  • Height (when whole)11.6 m
  • What still standsabout 6 m
  • Wreckage lies out to19 m away

The top half snapped off and fell to the front right. Two floors still stand, the second broken down in a slope where the top tore away, with a ledge of the third floor at the back (the stairs still reach it). The tower's top lies on its side out on the cave floor, a curved piece of wall beside it, and the fire bowl rolled furthest. Two chests (one up on the ledge) and three rock beetles.

Rebuild in wood

    The stone ground floor and second floor stay (worn walls too, unless something new goes on top of one). The broken walls, the third floor, the gallery and the roof come back in wood; the battlements become wooden railings. The fire bowl stays stone and iron (a wooden one would burn).

    Rebuild in stone

      Worn walls are rebuilt too, so every gap closes. Either way the wreckage is cleared and you learn both blueprints, the Wooden Spire and the Lantern Spire, plus 300 building XP.

      What makes it different

      The top floor is an open lantern gallery: pillars and railings all round, so the fire on the roof can be seen from far off. A new fire bowl stands on the battlement roof (stone pedestal, iron bowl, coals); its fire burns in the game.

      Few windows: two narrow ones per floor, none beside the door. The stairs climb a different side on every floor, their feet on open floor.

      No flat base space round it (your pick): it's a landmark, and the wreckage lies where the base space would be.

      "Mark new pieces" glows orange on every piece the rebuild adds.

The Twin Gate

The sixth ruin and the first grand one: a fortified gate between two stone towers, guarding the way deeper down. Behind it lies a walled practice yard and the guards' barracks. Left: rebuilt with root planks. Middle: rebuilt in cut stone, as it first stood. Right: the ruin as you find it.

What you find

  • Two towers10 × 10 m, 3 floors + top
  • Left tower's topring room + lantern room, 14.5 m
  • Gate house12 × 7 m, passage + gate room
  • Barracks30 × 8 m, 3 floors + kitchen
  • Whole placeabout 34 × 38 m

The right tower cracked in half: its outer half fell outward and lies past it in two big wall pieces and rubble, so you can look into all three floors. The gate room's right roof, the right barracks wing's roof and the kitchen's roof fell in. Part of the left yard wall is down. The doors are gone and the outer gate's two leaves were torn off; they lie past the grand steps. The left tower still stands and its fire still burns on top. The guards' things are still there: bunks, weapon racks, a mess table, the sergeants' muster table, the captain's chair, kitchen pieces, shield pells and braziers in the yard (42 pieces from the decor set). Three chests (guard room, captain's room, lantern room) and four rock beetles.

Rebuild in wood

    Everything comes back in planks; whole and worn stone walls stay (unless something new goes on top of one). The new stone trims come back in stone.

    Rebuild in stone

      Worn walls are rebuilt too, so every gap closes. Either way the wreckage is cleared and you learn both blueprints, the Wooden Twin Gate and the Stonehold Twin Gate, plus building XP.

      Your call: the price

      Building the whole Twin Gate from nothing in stone costs 14,758 cut stone, 1,397 mortar, 57 copper ingots and 4 blue glow crystals (1,221 pieces). The Outpost, our biggest so far, costs 8,340 cut stone. So this is about 1¾ times the Outpost.

      New models (not game pieces yet): brackets under every floor that juts out, trim bands along the floor lines with corner blocks, buttresses at the walls' feet, and a turret on every corner of both towers. Their price is still open, so they aren't in the rebuild cost.

      "Mark new pieces" glows orange on every piece the rebuild adds.

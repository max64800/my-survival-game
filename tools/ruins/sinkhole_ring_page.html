Sinkhole Ring

Ruin 14, a great round stone tower built up out of a deep hole in the cave floor. Every building is stone at the bottom and wood on top. In the ruin the bridge to the tower has fallen into the hole, so you climb down the hole's walls, go in at the bottom, and climb up through the tower to the lookout on top. Press "The whole blueprint" to see it as it was built.

The parts

  • The holeabout 28 × 24 m and 9 m deep, with a rock ledge half way down its right and back walls, and a high rock shelf behind it. All the cave's own rock, not built
  • Great Tower12-sided, 15 m across, four stone storeys from the hole's floor up to the rim. The Well Hall at the bottom with a round door at the back, stairs in a different spot each storey, a flat roof terrace with battlements
  • Keep and Crownon the terrace: a square stone keep (two storeys), and on top the wooden Crown, 2 m wider on three sides on stone brackets. A railed deck on its roof
  • Lookouta small wooden room on the deck with a steep 4-sided roof: the highest point. The best chest goes up here
  • Gatehouseon the rim: two stone storeys, wide steps up to its round main door with a lantern each side, a flat roof with battlements and a little wooden watch hut on one corner. Its back door opens onto the Gate Bridge
  • Gate Bridgea short stone bridge from the gatehouse to the tower's terrace (fallen into the hole in the ruin)
  • Pit stairswooden stairs pinned to the hole's walls: down the right wall to the ledge, along the ledge, then down the back wall to the hole's floor
  • Ledge Houseon the ledge: a stone storey, a wooden storey with a little balcony, a steep roof that peeks out over the rim
  • High Bridge and Winch Housea long wooden bridge from the Crown's back door over the hole to the Winch House on the high shelf: a stone storey and a wider wooden one with a gentle roof

Every door is round, no ladders. Every stair, door, railing and bracket is checked by the game's tests. Made only from pieces the game has.

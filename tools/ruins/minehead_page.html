Mine-head Works

Ruin 12, an old mine head on a cave floor. Every building is stone at the bottom and wood on top. Over the shaft stands a tall wooden headframe with the new Head Wheel on top. A trestle runs from it, over a dip, to the ore shed on a lower ledge. Press "The whole blueprint" to see it as it was built.

The parts

  • Headframe8 × 8 m over the shaft. A stone collar round the hole, then 3 tiers of braced wooden posts: the loading deck at 5 m (two flights of stairs up from the front), a 4 × 4 tower up to the top deck at 11.5 m (a ladder up), and the wheel stand with the Head Wheel on two bearing beams under a little roof (about 21 m up)
  • Engine house10 × 8 m, its back in the rock. A tall stone hall (two walls high) with a round front door and stairs up to its roof; on the roof a stone terrace with battlements and a wooden storey with a sloped roof
  • Stacka 6-sided stone chimney, 17 m tall, with 3 stone bands, in a hollow of the rock wall
  • Boiler shedstone floor, wooden walls, a lean-to roof that is high on the stack side
  • Trestlea railed wooden bridge at 5 m on 3 braced post frames, from the loading deck over the dip to the ore shed's loft
  • Ore shedon the lower ledge, 12 × 8 m: a tall stone ground storey, a wooden storey with 3 ore chutes under its wide windows, a wooden loft and a sloped roof. Steps in the rock lead down to its side door
  • Shaft and mine rooman 8 m shaft with ladders down to a mine room held up by wooden mine props. Press "Down the shaft" or "See underground"

8 flights of stairs and 8 ladders. Every stair, door and railing is checked by the game's tests. Made only from pieces the game has, plus the new Head Wheel. The dip, the ledge, the shaft and the mine room are dug by the world, not built. The cave needs to be about 22 m tall here.

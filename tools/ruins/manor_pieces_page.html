Hearth Manor: 5 New Brick Pieces

The manor needs balconies and more roofs, so it needs a few pieces Hearthbrick doesn't have yet. They glow blue. The little house on the right is only a test bench to show them in use; the manor itself comes next.

1. Bracket

  • Size0.5 × 1 m, sticks out 0.8 m

Holds up a floor that sticks out past the wall (a balcony). A rounded limestone foot, then 5 rows of bricks, each row reaching a bit further out (like an upside-down staircase), and a limestone cap on top.

2. Trim band + 3. its corner

  • Size2 m long, 0.4 m tall

A stripe that wraps the house where the upper floor starts, so tall walls don't look flat. Limestone, a row of little brick “teeth”, bricks, and a limestone top. The corner block joins two bands round a corner.

4. Short railing (1 m)

Half a normal railing, with one little arch instead of three. A balcony is 1 m deep, so its two ends need a 1 m railing.

5. Roof window

Sits on a steep roof. A brick front with a round leaded window, its own little tile roof with ridge tiles, dark wooden boards on its sides. The manor's roofs get several of these.

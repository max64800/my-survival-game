Hurt screen: 6 new options

What the screen does when your dwarf gets hurt. Everything here is painted with rough ink edges, like our hand-painted models, not smooth computer glows. The middle of the screen always stays clear.

Press Hit me! or leave "Auto hits" on. Try a light and a heavy hit, a hit from the left, and pull Health down low to see the "badly hurt" look. Tell me the number you like (or mix two, like "3 with 5's heartbeat").

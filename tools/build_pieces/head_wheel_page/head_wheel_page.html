The Head Wheel

F899, ruin 12 "Mine-head Works", step 1. The big wooden wheel that sits on top of the mine's headframe. It is a new Shack piece in the build menu (Supports, workbench level 2, 12 planks + 2 copper bars). Here it rests on two plain grey beams 4 m apart, the way it will sit on the headframe.

Things to look at

  • The rim is 16 chunky wood blocks with a groove for the rope between two cheek rings. Iron plates and pegs hold the cheeks.
  • 8 hewn spokes go into a round hub log with two iron hoops.
  • An 8-sided iron axle runs right through. At each end a wooden bearing block with an iron strap sits on a beam.
  • In the game you aim at one beam: the wheel puts one bearing on it and the other 4 m away, on the side you aim at.
  • It only looks like it works: it does not turn and there is no lift (your choice).

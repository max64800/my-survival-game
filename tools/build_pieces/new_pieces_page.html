Seven new building pieces

Kinds of piece that big survival builders use and our kit doesn't have yet. Each one stands beside real pieces (2 m walls, 2 m floors, 2 m stairs) so you can see its size. Names and looks are our own.

Sand colour = rough sketch Painted = real game piece

The proposals

  1. 1Corner stairs and spiral stairsStairs that turn, so towers can climb inside a small room. Corner stairs turn a quarter; spiral stairs go round a post in one 2 × 2 m spot.2 pieces · all three kits
  2. 2Stair railingA railing that slants along the stairs, for grand staircases and so nobody falls off the side.all three kits
  3. 3Upside-down slanted wallHangs from a ceiling and fills the space under overhangs. Two side by side make a pointed arch (shown with a floor on top).all three kits
  4. 4Thin edge stripA narrow lip along a floor's edge, so upper floors get a neat trim line. Right floor has it, left floor doesn't.all three kits
  5. 5Roof peak capCloses the tip where 4 slanted roofs meet, with a little knob on top.Shack and Hearthbrick only (Stonehold roofs are flat)
  6. 6Round floor hatchA floor with a round trapdoor, so you can climb between floors with a ladder. Round, like all our doors.all three kits
  7. 7Open lattice wallA see-through screen wall: you can look and shoot through it, but not walk through.all three kits

Your call

Tell me yes or no for each number (for example: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 no…"). Change anything you like too, such as size or shape.

Only the yes pieces get real models, hand-built and painted like the workbenches, in each kit's own design. These sketches are thrown away.

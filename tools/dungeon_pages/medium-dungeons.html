Medium stone dungeons

Draft for dungeons #5 to #9 of zone 1, built from Stonehold stone inside rock you can't dig. Each has about 20 spaces over 2-3 levels (12-15 minutes), a mini boss near the end whose key opens the treasure room, two deadly traps, 4-5 other traps and 3-4 hidden things. Each teaches one of Copper Helm, Mail, Greaves, Shield and Sword (shuffled per world). No water anywhere. Every one ends the same way (owner, Nov 20): beat the mini boss, its key opens the treasure room (recipe stand + big chest), and a way out runs back to the barred gate you saw in the first room. A lever opens it from inside.

Side views, as on the small dungeons page: each level has its main path in the middle, side rooms above and below it. Higher up the picture means higher in the cave.

Rune CallerKayKit mage. Stays back and shoots glowing runes from balconies and ledges.

All four are dwarf-sized skeletons of the dwarves who once lived here, repainted in our style. The mini bosses and the pack leaders are bigger, named versions.

A bit different in every world (picked by the world seed, the same for everyone in co-op): half the worlds mirror the dungeon left to right, side wings can swap places, hidden things, chests and small traps (bear jaws, tripwires, trapped chests) move between 2-3 spots, and carts and blades use one of 3 timing patterns. The main path, the deadly traps, the key, the boss and the recipe stand never move.

Traps: the Mossgate Hold kit made in stone, plus new ones: collapsing stairs, rolling boulder, pendulum blades and bells, push wall, spiked flail, spear wall, tripwire crossbow, bear jaws, portcullis and trapped chests. Boulders and flails throw you like a rag doll. The creatures come from the KayKit Skeletons pack, being made on the PC. Your call on everything here: names, rooms, traps, which creatures.

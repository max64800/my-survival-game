Medium stone dungeons

Draft for dungeons #5 to #9 of zone 1, built from Stonehold stone inside rock you can't dig. Each has about 20 spaces over 2-3 levels (12-15 minutes), a key that opens a locked door, a mini boss halfway, two deadly traps, 4-5 other traps and 3-4 hidden things. Each teaches one of Copper Helm, Mail, Greaves, Shield and Sword (shuffled per world). No water anywhere.

Side views, as on the small dungeons page: each level has its main path in the middle, side rooms above and below it. Higher up the picture means higher in the cave.

Bone ThrallKayKit minion. Weak, comes in groups, the filler in every room.
Bone WardenKayKit warrior. Armour and a shield, slow and tough. Hit it from the side.
Rune CallerKayKit mage. Stays back and shoots glowing runes from balconies and ledges.
Grave CreeperKayKit rogue. Fast, sneaks round to hit you from behind.

All four are dwarf-sized skeletons of the dwarves who once lived here, repainted in our style. The mini bosses and the leaders at the recipe stand are bigger, named versions.

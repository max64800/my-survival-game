Small wooden dungeons

Draft for dungeons #2, #3 and #4 of zone 1. They join Mossgate Hold (#1, being built on the PC). Each is now about Mossgate's size (13-14 spaces, 8-10 minutes), with at least one deadly trap, 4-5 traps in all and 3 hidden things, and built from root wood inside rock you can't dig. Each one teaches one of Copper Axe, Crossbow, Spear and Copper-shod Boots (shuffled per world). No water anywhere.

Each picture is a side view (each level has its main path on top and side rooms below it) cut through the rock. Higher up the picture means higher in the cave.

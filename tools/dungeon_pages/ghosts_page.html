How it fights

It floats a hand above the floor and swings a ghostly silver mace with the dwarf's own mace moves.

Every time it loses a quarter of its health, it fades into mist, a trail drifts 3 to 5.5 m away, and it comes back there and rushes at you, nearly twice as fast for 2 seconds.

When it dies it rises a little and fades away. No bones, no stone statue.

On this page

The ghost holds still here with its mace held low. Press Float to you to watch it glide at you slowly, the way it comes for you in the dungeon, and swing.

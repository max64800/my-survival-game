How it fights

It floats a hand above the floor and swings a ghostly silver mace with the dwarf's own mace moves.

Every time it loses a quarter of its health, it breaks into lots of small glowing dots that fly 3 to 5.5 m away and gather again, and it comes back there and rushes at you, nearly twice as fast for 2 seconds.

When it dies it rises a little and breaks into dots that drift up and fade. No bones, no stone statue.

On this page

The ghost holds still here with its mace held low. Press Float to you to watch it glide at you slowly, the way it comes for you in the dungeon, and swing.

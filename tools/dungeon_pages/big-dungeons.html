Big clay dungeons

Draft for dungeons #10 to #12 of zone 1: three old forge halls built from Hearthbrick clay brick. Each has about 30 spaces over 3 levels (20-25 minutes), mini bosses near the end whose keys open the treasure room, 3 deadly traps and medieval forge traps: tipping braziers, swinging anvils, push walls, pendulum blades, a rolling anvil, the great press, 3-lane ore carts, and molten metal kept in stone channels so it can never spill. Each teaches one of Copper Mace, Two-handed Axe and Two-handed Mace (shuffled per world). Every one ends the same way (owner, Nov 20): beat the mini boss (Hammerfall: both), its key opens the treasure room (recipe stand + big chest), and a way out runs back to the one-way exit you saw in the first room: a barred gate with a lever on the inside, or a high ledge you jump down from onto a soft heap. It can be on any side.

Every trap has a clue you can see or hear, and a safe way through: time it, bait it and step back, or shoot its chain or switch with a crossbow. The table says how for each one.

A bit different in every world (picked by the world seed, the same for everyone in co-op): half the worlds mirror the dungeon left to right, side wings can swap places, hidden things, chests and small traps (bear jaws, tripwires, trapped chests) move between 2-3 spots, and carts and blades use one of 3 timing patterns. The main path, the deadly traps, the key, the boss and the recipe stand never move.

Traps use the Mossgate Hold kit made in brick, plus the new forge traps (tipping braziers, swinging anvils and crucibles, push walls, the rolling anvil, the great press, molten channels, ore carts). Anvils, carts and flails throw you like a rag doll. The creatures come from the KayKit Skeletons pack, being made on the PC. Your call on everything here: names, rooms, traps, which creatures.

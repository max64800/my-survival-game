Big clay dungeons

Draft for dungeons #10 to #12 of zone 1: three old forge halls built from Hearthbrick clay brick. Each has about 30 spaces over 3 levels (20-25 minutes), keys and locked doors, mini bosses, 3 deadly traps and the forge's own machines as traps: fire jets, swinging hammers, push pistons, rolling weights, and molten metal kept in stone channels so it can never spill. Each teaches one of Copper Mace, Two-handed Axe and Two-handed Mace (shuffled per world).

Every trap has a clue you can see or hear, and a safe way through: time it, bait it and step back, or shoot its chain or switch with a crossbow. The table says how for each one.

All four are dwarf-sized skeletons of the dwarves who once lived here, repainted in our style. The mini bosses and the leaders at the recipe stand are bigger, named versions.

## About this game
**The kingdom fell. The dwarves never did.**

You wake with stone crumbling off your skin and no memory of how you got here. Around you lie the ruins of a great dwarf
kingdom: your ancestors' home, silent for ages. Somewhere far below lies the answer to what happened, and a way to break
the curse.

**Dig anywhere**
Carve your own tunnels through moss, stone and copper ore. Every cave is different, and some ceilings glow like a night sky.

**Keep your lantern lit**
Your lantern runs on glowing crystals. Run out, and the dark gets close.

**Build a home in the deep**
Three building styles: a rough log shack, a stone keep with battlements, or a warm brick cottage ⚠ (all three in the build
menu by launch). Snap together walls, floors, round doors and roofs.

**Craft and survive**
Gather roots, mushrooms and ore. Smelt copper, cook hearty stews, and forge better tools and armour.

**Face what lives below**
Rock Beetles that charge, Rock Snails that hide in their shells, Cave Bats that dive from the dark ⚠ (all three in the
game). The deeper you go, the stranger and more cursed they become.

**Uncover the past**
Old runes carved into the walls hold memories of the kingdom before it fell ⚠ (rune memories planned; keep only if they're
in by launch). Follow them to the truth.

**Dig deeper together**
Play alone or with up to 3 friends in online co-op ⚠.

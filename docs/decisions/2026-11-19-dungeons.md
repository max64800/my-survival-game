# Dungeons dug into the rock: decisions (owner, Nov 19, 2026)

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| How many | 8-10 small dungeons in Zone 1 (Mossy Hollows) | owner: "lots" | medium/large sizes are added |
| Entrance | a round stone door in the wall of a cave; all rooms carved inside solid rock behind it | owner's brief | - |

| Maps | hand-made, 5+ maps; each dungeon flips/turns one and shuffles chests, creatures and some traps | owner picked 5+ for variety | - |
| Order of work | map 1 first (small size), owner OKs the sketch before any game code | owner's brief | - |
| End guard | a big pack with a pack leader, no boss | owner | - |
| Traps | a mix: some hurt a lot, some kill you instantly. Every trap gives a clue (cracked plate, wall holes, sound) | owner | co-op deaths feel unfair |

| Refill | creatures move back in after a while; the big chest stays empty | owner | - |
| Placement rule | nothing the world grows (columns, rocks, crystals, roots, plants) inside a dungeon or in its doorway, with a test | CLAUDE.md placement rule | - |

## Round 2 (owner, Nov 19, after the map 1 sketch)

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building style | root wood (Delver's Shack, `plank_root`) walls and floors in every room and corridor; room 9 is a tall natural cave with a stalactite ceiling | owner: "lived in by other dwarves" | - |
| Breaking | furniture can be smashed (a little root wood + small finds); walls can't | no digging around traps | - |
| Furniture | our existing furniture is used to make rooms look lived-in | owner | - |
| Falling stalactites | fall when you walk under (or onto) them; always 50 HP; a crossbow bolt can shoot one down first | owner | max health grows a lot |
| Chasm | a very long drop onto spikes: instant death. Rock beetles live around it | owner | - |
| Grave spot | every death, everywhere: the grave goes where you stood safely about 3 s before you died | owner picked the recommendation | - |
| Main prize | recipes (weapons, armor, food, drinks, tools, gear, furniture, decor) + gold. Ruins give building blueprints; dungeons give recipes | owner | - |
| Loop | the way in is the way out. After the final rooms you come back into the entrance room by dropping down a ledge or opening a gate/door from the far side | owner (like classic fantasy dungeons) | - |
| Grid | everything on the 2 m building grid, in dungeons AND ruins; round rooms/towers only join on their one flat side | owner | - |
| Traps hurt creatures | yes, you can lure creatures into traps | owner | - |
| Creatures | only what we have now (spiders, bats, snails, rock beetles); new dungeon creatures later | owner | new creatures are made |
| Not chosen | "fair danger" and "no empty dead ends" are not rules (left out by the owner) | - | - |

## Open / deferred
- How long until creatures come back: **assumed** 3 in-game days; decided when we build refills.
- Dying in a dungeon: **assumed** the normal grave, where you died (an instant-kill trap leaves the grave next to it, never inside the trap).
- Medium and large sizes: later.
- Rare finds list (which blueprints/gems): decided when the loot tables are made.

Ruin 15: the Pillar Spiral

A very simple shape model, not real pieces yet. It's the plan, shown in plain blocks.

Stonehold stone Hearthbrick brick Shack wood Tile roofs Shingle roofs The way

The idea

A giant rock pillar stands from the cave floor all the way up to the ceiling, 25 m tall. Dwarves built a round stone hall around its foot. Then they built wooden stairs that wind up around the pillar like the stripe on a candy cane, with one house on each side. Each house is a storey taller than the one before.

It's the first ruin with all three materials: stone at the bottom (flat roofs), brick in the middle (tile roofs), wood on top (shingle roofs).

It's the first ruin you climb round and round. Press Show the way to see it, and Close on the spiral to look at the stairs.

The parts

  • Grand stair + Ring Hall. A wide stair up to a big round door with lanterns. Behind it, a round stone hall 22 m wide that wraps all the way around the pillar's foot. Its flat roof is a terrace with battlements.
  • The Spiral. Wooden stairs hugging the rock: up one floor on the right side, one at the back, one on the left, one at the front. A flat landing at every corner.
  • Lamp House (right). Brick, 2 storeys, the lowest roof.
  • Store House (back). Brick, with a wooden top storey sticking out on brackets. Its back door opens onto a little bridge.
  • Bunk House (left). Brick, 3 storeys, a wooden top storey on brackets.
  • Bell Tower (front, right over the main door). The tallest: 4 brick storeys, a wider wooden storey, then the Eyrie, an 8-sided lookout under a pointed roof near the ceiling. The best chest is here.
  • Arch Bridge + Kiln House. A stone bridge from the terrace over a crack in the floor to a brick kiln house with two chimneys on a rock lump.
  • Shrine Bridge + Rock Shrine. From the Store House to a small stone shrine dug into the cave wall on a high rock shelf.

Built into the cave

The pillar goes right through the middle. Five ground levels: the crack, the low floor in front, the cave floor, the rock lump, the high shelf. Two bridges cross gaps, the shrine is dug into the wall, and the Eyrie sits just under the ceiling.

Lessons kept

Every door opens onto a landing, a terrace or a bridge at its own height. Nothing hangs over the main door. No ramps to windows, no ladders: stairs all the way, each with free floor at the top and bottom.

Heights

Ring Hall 4.5 m, Kiln House 10 m, Lamp House 11 m, Rock Shrine 12 m, Store House 13.5 m, Bunk House 16 m, Bell Tower 22 m. The ceiling is at 25 m.

When it's a ruin

The left stairs of the spiral have fallen, so you climb up through the Bunk House inside instead. The Eyrie's roof leans, the bell lies on the terrace, a chimney is broken, and rocks from the ceiling lie on the terrace. Rebuild it to learn the blueprint.

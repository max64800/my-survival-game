Ruin 11: Brickcrown Keep

A very simple shape model, not real pieces yet. It's the plan, shown in plain blocks.

Stonehold stone Hearthbrick brick Brick roof tiles

The idea

Long ago, dwarves built a stone fort here. Later, brickmakers moved in. They mended the cracks and built new storeys on top in red brick. Stone at the bottom, brick on top, and stone bands at every floor line tie the two together.

Press Old stone fort only to see the fort before the brickmakers came.

The parts

  • Terrace + grand stair (stone). A wide step up to the round front door.
  • Keep (stone, then brick). Two stone storeys with a flat top. The brick storey on top sticks out 1 m on brackets. The one above steps back in and leaves a railed terrace. A sloped tile roof and a brick chimney sit on top. The back of the keep goes into the cave's rock wall.
  • Left tower (stone, then brick). A round stone tower. Its brick top is a bit thinner, so there's a walkway round it. A pointed brick roof finishes it.
  • Curtain wall (stone). A thick wall with a walkway and battlements on top. One old crack was patched with brick.
  • Gate (new). A round-topped gate with two wooden doors in the curtain wall. A stone bridge crosses the dip in front of it.
  • Courtyard (new). Carved back into the cave rock behind the gate, with a well. The rock hangs over its back.
  • Rooms in the rock (new). On the courtyard's left, the keep's stone base carries on back into the rock: a stone front with two round doors and windows, rooms behind it.
  • Right tower (all stone). The oldest part, never rebuilt. It stands on a rock lump and has a flat top with battlements, the way Stonehold roofs must be. New: a short wall, the same height as the curtain wall, joins its back to the rock.

Heights

Terrace 1.5 m, curtain wall 7 m, left tower 16 m, right tower 13 m (on its 3 m rock), keep roof 19 m. The cave needs about 21 m here.

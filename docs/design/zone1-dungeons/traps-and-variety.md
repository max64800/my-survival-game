# Zone 1 dungeons: traps, assets and variety

*Nov 20, the owner's trap picks. The three pages and files 02-12 were redrawn to match (same day).*

## Owner's trap decisions (Nov 20)
- **Out:** fire jets (too futuristic), gas valve, steam bellows, pistons, swinging hooks, coin press, sinking blocks.
- **Ragdoll:** being hit by a mine cart, a swinging boulder or a spiked flail throws you as a floppy body
  (everyone in co-op sees it, so friends can clip it).
- Every trap keeps a **clue** and a **safe way through**.

## Variety: same dungeon, a bit different in each world
Picked from the world seed, so everyone in a co-op game gets the same layout. Refills keep the same layout.
- **Mirror:** half of the worlds flip the dungeon left↔right. Signs and runes keep facing the right way.
- **Swapping wings:** side wings marked "swap" trade places (the hidden vault may be on the left branch or the right).
- **Hidden things move:** each dungeon has 5-6 possible hidden spots (buried chest under the floor, cracked wall,
  hidden boulder, secret lever) and the seed picks 3. A hidden room may sit behind one of 2 places.
- **Chests move:** each chest has 2-3 possible spots.
- **Small traps move:** bear jaws, tripwires, dart plates and trapped chests have 2-3 possible spots each.
- **Cart and blade patterns:** the triple carts and pendulums use one of 3 timing patterns.
- **Never changes:** the main path, the room order, the signature and deadly traps (their rooms are built round
  them), the key, the mini boss, the recipe stand. Players can still say "the cart room" and mean the same thing.

# #10 Emberdeep Forge

> **Traps changed Nov 20:** see [traps-and-variety.md](traps-and-variety.md). It wins over the trap notes below.

*Draft, Nov 20 (Mac, overnight). Generated from the dungeon pages; edit the page data and run gen_docs again.*

The great forge goes down and down: workshops at the top, the cooling and quench halls below, and the deep furnace at the bottom where it all still glows.

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | big, 30 spaces, 3 level(s), 20-25 min |
| Built from | Hearthbrick clay brick (`clay_brick`), inside old dwarf stone (can't be dug) |
| Floor to ceiling | 6 m in rooms, more in tall halls |
| Teaches one of | Copper Mace, Two-handed Copper Axe, Two-handed Copper Mace (shuffled per world) |
| Way out | An old ore lift in the Heart of Emberdeep carries you all the way up to the Ember Gate. |

## Loot

- Side chest: 4-8 Rusty Scrap, 8-15 gold, 2-4 Bandages or 8 Metal Bolts, 40% a cooked food
- Hidden chest: 15-25 gold, 3-5 Copper Ingots, 40% a rune, 8% a gem
- Mini boss: drops its key, 10-15 gold
- Recipe stand + big chest: this dungeon's page, 30-50 gold, 35% an Old Blueprint the group doesn't know yet, 12% a gem
- Everything refills 1 hour of play after it was taken (DungeonLoot).

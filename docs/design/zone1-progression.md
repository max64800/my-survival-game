# Zone 1 progression (owner's choices, November 15, 2026)

Goal: Zone 1 (Mossy Hollows) takes a new player **3-5 hours** from start to beating its boss.
It should be a struggle but feel rewarding, like Valheim: copper is a real milestone, not something
you trip over in the first cave. This page replaces anything older that disagrees
(`progression-and-economy.md` §3.1 and §4.1 still describe the old copper-in-the-first-cave order).

## The ladder
1. **Bare hands and loose things** → rootwood (thin roots), loose stones, fiber.
2. **Stone tools** (by hand): Stone Pickaxe, then the Workbench, rope, Stone Sword, Shield, Build Hammer.
   Wood buildings from the start (Workbench).
2b. **The ruined tower: first stop and first home** (owner, Nov 19). It comes with a **Builder's Chest**: put wood
   planks in it and it patches the tower's holes with wood. A repaired tower is a ready first base (campfire + bed
   give the Cozy rest buff). The tower's chests hold a **rune memory** that points to the **first of the 3 wall
   markings**: the tower starts the trail to the boss.
3. **Workbench level 2** (Chopping Block next to it) → **Fang Pickaxe** (Stone Pickaxe + 4 bat fangs + 1 leather).
   You have to hunt bats and tan a hide. Stone buildings need a Mason's Table.
4. **The Copper Deeps**: one or two far, low, dark caverns in Mossy Hollows, guarded by tougher beetles.
   The only copper veins in Zone 1, and **only the Fang Pickaxe (or better) can mine them**. The stone pickaxe
   bounces off: "Copper is too hard for stone. Make a Fang Pickaxe (Workbench level 2)."
5. **Copper**: Smelter → copper ingots → Anvil, copper tools (**Copper Pickaxe from copper only, no boss item**), Cooking Pot, Gem Bench. Workbench level 3 (copper-free
   Tool Rack: planks + rope), level 4 (Carpenter's Vise: needs copper). Brick needs a level-2 Mason's Table.
6. **The boss, Orrumhal**: find the 3 wall markings that point to its lair. The lair is **sealed with hard stone
   that only copper tools can break** (owner, Nov 19): the Fang Pickaxe bounces off. Inside, summon it with an offering of
   **5 copper ingots + 3 bat fangs + 2 glow glands**. You must reach the Deeps AND hunt.
7. Beating it gives the **Keeper's Heartstone**. Copper Pickaxe + Heartstone at the Anvil = **Keeper's Pickaxe**
   (owner, Nov 19), the only pickaxe that breaks the border rock → Zone 2.

## What makes you stronger in Zone 1
Station levels (owner's pick): better gear needs a better-equipped Workbench / Mason's Table / Anvil.
(Not chosen for now: creature-part armor, flint tools, food buffs.)

Ruin 14: the Sinkhole Ring

A very simple shape model, not real pieces yet. It's the plan, shown in plain blocks.

Stonehold stone Shack wood Shingle roofs The way

The idea

Dwarves found a deep round hole in the cave floor (a sinkhole, 9 m deep). They built a huge round stone tower up from its bottom, so its top rises out past the edge. Houses cling to the pit's walls, stairs are pinned to the rock, and two bridges reach across to the tower.

Every building is stone below, wood above. Stone parts have flat roofs, wood parts sloped shingle roofs.

It's the first ruin you go down into. The front bridge has fallen into the pit, so you climb down the walls, go in at the bottom, and climb up the tower's inside to the top. Press Show the way to see it, and Cut the pit open to look into the hole from the front.

The parts

  • Gatehouse + grand stair. A wide stair up to a stone gatehouse with a big round door and lanterns. A little wooden watch hut sits on one corner of its flat roof (on purpose, so it isn't a perfect mirror).
  • Gate Bridge. A stone bridge from the gatehouse to the tower's roof terrace. In the ruin its middle lies at the bottom of the pit.
  • Great Tower, round base. 15 m wide, 12-sided, from the bottom of the pit up past the edge. At the bottom, the Well Hall with an old dry well and a chest. Its flat top is a terrace with battlements.
  • The Keep, the Crown, the Lookout. On the terrace: a square stone keep (steps in), a wider wooden storey on brackets (sticks out), a balcony, then an 8-sided wooden lookout (steps in) under a tall pointed roof. The best chest is in the lookout. The tower changes shape 4 times.
  • Pit stairs. Wooden stairs pinned to the pit's right wall, down to a rock ledge halfway.
  • Ledge House. On the ledge: a stone room dug into the rock, a wooden storey sticking out on brackets, a little balcony. Its roof just peeks out over the edge of the pit.
  • Back-wall stairs. Down the pit's back wall to the bottom, then round to the tower's back door.
  • High Bridge + Winch House. A long wooden bridge from the Crown to a house on a high rock shelf. A wooden arm sticks out of the house with a rope and bucket hanging all the way down into the pit.

Built into the cave

Five ground levels: the pit's bottom, the ledge, the cave floor, the terrace, the high shelf. Two houses have their backs in the rock, two bridges cross the pit, and the stairs hug its walls.

Lessons kept

Every door has steps up to it or a floor level with it. Nothing hangs over a door. No ramps to windows, no ladders: stairs all the way, each with free floor at the top and bottom.

Heights

Above the cave floor: Ledge House roof 3 m, Gatehouse 7 m (hut 10 m), Winch House 14 m, tower 16.5 m. From the bottom of the pit the tower is 25 m tall.

When it's a ruin

The Gate Bridge has fallen in, the High Bridge sags, the lookout's roof leans, the watch hut is half gone, and a rock pillar grows up through the Well Hall floor. Rebuild it to learn the blueprint.

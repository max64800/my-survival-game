Ruin 12: Mine-head Works

A very simple shape model, not real pieces yet. It's the plan, shown in plain blocks.

Stonehold stone Shack wood Wood shingle roofs New piece: Head Wheel

The idea

Long ago, dwarves dug a deep mine here. A tall wooden frame stood over the hole, with a big wheel on top. A rope ran from a steam engine in the stone house, over the wheel and down the shaft, to pull ore carts up. Then carts rolled over a wooden bridge to the ore shed. One day the mine was left, and it fell apart.

Press See underground to look inside the rock at the mine room under the shaft.

The parts

  • Stone terrace + grand stair. One long stone step that the engine house and the frame both stand on. A wide stair leads up to the engine house's round door.
  • Engine house (stone). A tall hall for the engine, a smaller storey on top that steps back, flat roofs (Stonehold). Its back goes into the cave's rock wall. A tall stone stack (chimney) stands beside it.
  • Boiler shed (wood). A small lean-to on the engine house's left, with a sloped roof.
  • Headframe (wood on a stone collar). The tallest part. It changes shape 3 times: a wide base with a loading deck, a thinner braced middle, then a wide top deck on brackets, then the wheel under a small sloped roof.
  • The Head Wheel (new piece). The big wooden wheel on top. The rope runs from it to the engine house, and down the shaft.
  • The shaft. A square hole with a stone rim, about 8 m deep. Ladders go down its side.
  • Mine room (underground). At the bottom: a dug-out room held up by wooden props, the fallen lift cage, and the best chest. A tunnel at its end has caved in.
  • Trestle bridge (wood). A tall wooden bridge with cart rails, from the loading deck, over a dip in the cave floor, to the ore shed.
  • Ore shed (wood on stone). On a lower rock ledge, its back in the rock. Its top floor sticks out on brackets; chutes on its front poured ore into carts on the rails below.
  • Stairs in the rock. Steps cut into the slope between the upper floor and the lower ledge, with rock beside them.

Built into the cave

Backs in the rock (engine house, ore shed), two floor levels plus the mine room, a bridge over a dip, and stairs cut into the slope.

Heights

Terrace 1.5 m, boiler shed 4 m, engine house 8 m, ore shed 11 m (from its lower ledge), stack 17 m, headframe 20 m. Shaft 8 m down. The cave needs about 22 m here.

When it's a ruin

The wheel hangs crooked, the bridge's middle has fallen into the dip, the cage lies at the bottom of the shaft, and roofs have holes. Rebuild it to learn the blueprint.

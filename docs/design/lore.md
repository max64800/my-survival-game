# Lore (owner's choices, November 17, 2026)

Long-term story frame. Decided by the owner in three rounds of questions. "Open" items are decided later; don't fill them in.

## The story in one breath
You wake up, stone flaking off your skin. You were frozen in stone, and you have no memory of how or why. Around you lie the
ruins of a once-great dwarf kingdom: **your own ancestors' kingdom**. You and up to 3 friends (or alone) set out to find out
what happened and to **break the curse that turned the dwarves to stone**, and to rebuild.

## Decided
- **Start:** frozen in stone (like after a gorgon's gaze; our own idea, no Medusa), waking with no memory.
- **Ruins:** built by your own ancestors, a huge dwarf civilisation that fell.
- **Tone:** heroic adventure.
- **Who you are:** one of 4 friends (co-op) or alone. No backstory yet.
- **Goal:** find out what happened, break the curse, and rebuild.
- **How the story is told:** **rune memories**. Touching old wall runes shows short visions of the kingdom before it fell
  (few words, mostly pictures). This fits the existing wall-marking boss clues and the Rune Slab.
- **Frozen dwarves in the world:** only a few, hidden. Finding one is rare and special.
- **Monsters:** deeper = more cursed. Near the top they're cave creatures; deeper down they're changed by the curse
  (stone skin, glowing cracks, crystal growths). The crystal variants (frost, ember, spore) fit this.
- **Breaking the curse:** only at the very end; until then the stone dwarves stay stone.
- **Name direction:** about the dwarves/clan, short and punchy, with "Dwarf"/"Dwarves" in it.
  - Spelling: **Dwarves** (the fantasy spelling) for the plural, Dwarf for one.

## Open (decide later)
- What turned the dwarves to stone, and who or what did it.
- Your dwarf's own history.
- The kingdom's name.
- The final boss and The Abyss / Ancient Dwarf City zones' role in the story.

## Fits with what's already built
- Mossy Hollows has old dwarf ruins.
- The zone bosses are found through wall markings (Orrumhal in Mossy Hollows).
- The Rune Slab is the skill system.
- The world is a 7-zone star, with an "Ancient Dwarf City" above and "The Abyss" below.

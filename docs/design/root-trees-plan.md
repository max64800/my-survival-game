# Root Trees: the cave's trees (plan, owner Nov 18, 2026)

The owner chopped a giant root pillar in the first cave and loved it: "this is a perfect way to get trees". The pillars
become **Root Trees**: the main way to get a lot of wood, planted again with a seed. Status: **plan, waiting for the
owner to pick one of the 3 looks**.

## What the owner asked
- 2-3 Root Trees in the first cave, 1-2 in every big cave (at least 1).
- Better models (several), hand-built in Blender, painted with ChatGPT bark textures (variant sheets, MODEL-RULES Rule 6).
- A lot of wood each: about **30 rootwood**.
- When chopped completely, it **breaks or decays** (not "stripped bare" and still standing).
- **Plant it again**: a Root Seed, planted with the Mason's Shovel. After 30 minutes (real time) it's a small root
  sprout; 30 minutes later, while nobody is looking, it has grown all the way to the ceiling.

## How it works (all three looks share this)
1. **Placing** (cave plan, same seed = same trees): first cave 3, every big cave 1-2, where the ceiling is 5-14 m up;
   never on built ground, paths, places, stone pillars or bridges (the placement rule).
2. **Chopping**: every hit gives wood (axe 2, pickaxe 1) until 30 are taken. The tree shows it: deep cuts appear, and
   at 10 and 20 chops chunks of bark and root fall off (wood pieces bursting out, like the rock chunks).
3. **The end**: on the last chop the trunk cracks in the middle: the top half pulls back up into the ceiling and the
   bottom half crumbles into wood chunks that fade away, leaving a low stump. It drops **1-2 Root Seeds**.
4. **Planting**: hold a Root Seed, aim at floor with the Mason's Shovel (right click) where the ceiling is 5-14 m up and
   nothing is built: a little mound with a seedling appears.
5. **Growing**: 30 minutes: a waist-high root sprout (gives a few wood if chopped early). 30 minutes more: a full Root
   Tree up to the ceiling. Each step only happens when no player is looking at it (it swaps while out of view).
   Times count while the world is running (saved with the world).
6. **Co-op**: ask, host approves, everyone updates (chops, breaking, planting, growing). Saved: wood taken per tree,
   broken trees, planted trees and their planting time.

## Three looks (pick one; the picture: docs/design/pictures/root-trees-3-looks.png)
- **A. Braided Giant**: thick roots twisting round each other like a rope, from floor to ceiling, feet spreading out.
  (The current pillar, done properly: bigger roots, bark texture, moss in the grooves.)
- **B. Ancient Trunk**: one huge gnarled trunk with buttress roots fanning into the floor and a crown of roots
  spreading over the ceiling like an upside-down tree meeting a tree; knots, hollows, hanging root strands.
- **C. Glowing Root Cage**: a cage of roots with gaps you can see through, glowing fungus and soft light inside the
  hollow heart, moss on the bark.

Smeltery Terraces, version 2

A simple shape model, not real pieces yet: blocks for brick, brown for wood, dark for doors and windows. It shows the plan before I build it from our Hearthbrick pieces. Everything still sits on the 2 m grid, and every round tower meets a square part on only one flat side, so it can be built for real.

The parts

  • Vaults + terrace. The row of arched store rooms; the terrace on top. NEW: a lower front terrace and a split grand stair (two flights meeting at a landing).
  • Smelting hall. Tall first floor, jettied upper floor. NEW: a long wooden balcony on brackets across the front, and a round stair turret on its front-left corner.
  • Chimney tower. Square for 4 storeys, NEW: a wrap-round balcony halfway up, four little round corner turrets on brackets, then it turns 12-sided for a ring room, a crown of balconies, and a square chimney with a bronze cap on top.
  • Furnace house. NEW: a fat round bottom, a thinner round neck on top (like a kiln bottle), a railed deck where it steps in, and a square porch in front.
  • Bridge. NEW: roofed in wood, with a round pier in the dip holding up its middle.
  • Watch turret. NEW: a small round tower on the terrace's front-right corner with a balcony crown, so the right side isn't flat.

Heights

Vaults 3 m, watch turret 9 m, furnace 12 m, hall 14 m, tower 22 m. The cave needs about 24 m here.

Your call

Is this rich enough? Tell me what to add, take away or move. Once you OK the shapes, I build it from our real brick and wood pieces and show you that page.

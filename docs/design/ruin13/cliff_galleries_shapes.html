Ruin 13: the Cliff Galleries

A very simple shape model, not real pieces yet. It's the plan, shown in plain blocks.

Stonehold stone Hearthbrick brick Clay tile roofs The way up

The idea

Dwarves once lived up a tall rock wall in the cave. Their stone hall is dug into the foot of the cliff. Higher up, houses sit on natural rock ledges, and brick rooms and walkways hang out from the cliff on brackets (supports under them, wide at the top).

Every building is stone below, brick above. Stone parts have flat roofs, brick parts sloped tile roofs.

You climb it like a mountain. Press Show the way up to see the path from the door to the top.

The parts

  • Stone terrace + grand stair. A wide stair up to the big round main door, with lanterns each side.
  • Stone Hall (stone, then brick). A tall stone hall, its back dug into the cliff. On top, a brick storey sticks out 1 m on the left and right on brackets (not over the door). Behind it, a flat stone roof terrace against the rock.
  • The crack + bridge. A deep crack in the cave floor. A brick bridge crosses it, from the hall's brick storey to the ledge house.
  • Ledge House (stone, then brick). On a rock ledge 6 m up. A stone room, then a brick storey on brackets, sloped roof.
  • Cliff stairs. Brick stairs pinned to the cliff face, climbing left from the ledge house.
  • Hanging Gallery. A long brick walkway along the cliff, 13 m up, held by brackets in the rock and posts on the hall's roof.
  • Watch Tower (stone, then brick). The tallest part, on a tall rock shelf. It changes shape 3 times: a two-storey stone base, a wider brick storey on brackets, an 8-sided lookout, then a steep roof. The best chest waits in the lookout.
  • Hoist + store shed. A timber arm sticks out of the tower with a rope down to a small stone-and-brick shed at the bottom. The dwarves pulled goods up with it.

Built into the cave

Every building's back is in the rock. Three ground levels (cave floor, a ledge, a high shelf), a bridge over a crack, and stairs and a walkway along the cliff.

Mine-head lessons kept

Every door has steps up to it or a floor level with it. No ramp leads to a window. No ladders this time: stairs all the way.

Heights

Shed 3.5 m, Stone Hall 10 m, Ledge House 13 m, Watch Tower 26 m. The cave needs about 29 m here.

When it's a ruin

The gallery's middle has fallen onto the hall's roof, the bridge sags, the tower's roof leans, and the roofs have holes. Rebuild it to learn the blueprint.

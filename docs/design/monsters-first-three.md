# The first three creatures: Rock Snail, Cave Bat, Rock Beetle

*Decided by the owner on November 14, 2026 (multiple-choice session with Claude 3).
These replace the earlier regular monsters for now. The others stay in the code, hidden; the
bosses are unchanged. When this doc and `combat-and-monsters.md` disagree, this doc wins for these three.*

## The owner's words

> "The first ones should be snails. They're very slow, but they hit pretty hard, and if you hit them
> a couple of times they hide in their shell for a couple of seconds and take 90% less damage, so it
> takes some time to kill them. I like the bats. They have to be somewhat big, so it's easy to hit
> them. They fly in, attack, fly out, fly in again. I want some kind of beetle bug as well.
> Polish those three before we move to anyone else."

## Rock Snail (Mossy Hollows)

| | |
|---|---|
| Fight | Very slow (0.6 m/s walk). Hits hard: Shell Slam 20 (1.2 s warning), Lunging Bite 14. |
| Shell | After **3 hits** it pulls into its shell for **3 s**: **90% less damage**, doesn't move or attack. Then it comes out and the count starts again. |
| Numbers | HP 110, armor 20. Weak to blunt (×1.25), resists slash (×0.75). Packs of 1-2. 14 XP. |
| Drops | Snail meat (1-2), shell pieces (2, 80%). |
| Uses | Shell pieces → **Shell Shield**, **Shell armor set** (helmet, chest, boots). Meat → cooked at the campfire, or Snail & Mushroom Stew. |

## Cave Bat (roosts in caverns everywhere)

| | |
|---|---|
| Fight | Dog-sized, wings ~1.5 m, so it's easy to hit. Hangs from the ceiling asleep. Wakes, cruises ~3 m above you, **dives to bite (11)**, **climbs away ~7 m**, dives again. Comes 1-2 at a time. |
| Numbers | HP 45, no armor. Weak to pierce (×1.5, crossbows!) and slash (×1.25). 10 XP. |
| Drops | Wing leather (1, 90%), fangs (2, 70%). |
| Uses | Wing leather → leather (leather armor, satchel = the bigger bag, waterskin). Fangs → **Fang bolts** (crossbow), **Fanged Pickaxe** (digs faster). |

## Rock Beetle (small: Mossy Hollows) and Great Rock Beetle (big: Fungal Jungle)

| | |
|---|---|
| Fight | A **charger**: lowers its head (1.1 s warning) and charges **in a straight line** (it doesn't steer). Sidestep and it thunders past, then stands still a moment: hit its **soft back** for **double damage**. Hard stone shell in front. Also snaps with its mandibles up close. |
| Numbers | Rock Beetle: HP 80, armor 30, charge 18. Great Rock Beetle: HP 220, armor 45, charge 30 + knock-down. 15 / 45 XP. |
| Drops | Bug meat (2 / 4), glow gland (60% / 2 at 90%). |
| Uses | Glow gland → **lantern fuel** (lasts twice as long as a crystal), **glow bolts**, a **Glow Lamp** you can place. Meat → roasted at the campfire, or Bug & Mushroom Stew. |

## Food

| Dish | Where | From | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooked Snail | Campfire | 1 snail meat | Hunger +30, +10% max health (25 min) |
| Roasted Bug | Campfire | 1 bug meat | Hunger +30, +8% damage (25 min) |
| Snail & Mushroom Stew | Cooking pot | 2 snail meat, 2 hollowcap | Hunger +55, +20% max health (35 min) |
| Bug & Mushroom Stew | Cooking pot | 2 bug meat, 2 glowcap | Hunger +55, +20% max stamina (35 min) |

Raw meat can be eaten but may upset your stomach (like other raw meat).

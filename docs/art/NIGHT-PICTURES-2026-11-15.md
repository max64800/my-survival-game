## Prompt notes that worked
- Stations: "chunky hand-painted style like the workbenches, every part touching, nothing floating, no perfect circles,
  plain light-grey background, no text". Two objects per picture saves time.
- Weapons and creature parts got refused twice (words like "poison", "fang", "gland"). Saying **"cartoony fantasy game
  ITEM ICON sheet"** and gentler words fixed it.
- Item sheets: "12 items in a neat 4 x 3 grid, each on its own with space around it".
- ChatGPT sometimes shows two versions ("Which image do you like more?"); pick the one with more space around items.

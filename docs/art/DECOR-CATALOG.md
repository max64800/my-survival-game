# Decor catalogue (Nov 22, 2026): every piece and exactly where it goes

Every decor model from the decor night (the `tools/asset_forge/builds/decor_nov21_*.py` files): **530 models**, plus
12 looted `_empty` twins (named in their full model's Notes). Nothing here is placed in the game yet: the owner looks at
this list first. Rules for decor: `docs/art/DECOR-BRIEF-NOV21.md`. Placement rule: `CLAUDE.md` (nothing grown inside
built things; keep paths, doors and stairs free).

Every piece has its own place (owner, Nov 22: "so when we're placing all of that, we know where to place them
correctly"). How to read a row:
- **Place**: the dungeon, ruin or home ("also:" = it fits a second place too). **Room / spot**: the numbered room from
  that dungeon's Rooms table in `docs/design/zone1-dungeons/` (Mossgate Hold has no table yet: its numbers come from
  the game's map, `src/world/dungeon_map.gd`), or the ruin floor, and where in the room. Home pieces go in the build
  menu; their room is the kind of room the player builds (kitchen, bedroom, workshop...).
- **Mount** (how it stands), one of: floor, middle / floor, back to a wall / floor, in a corner / wall, hung (its
  bottom this high) / ceiling, hanging (this far down) / on top of another piece / outside, on the ground / in a niche /
  in an opening (fills a gap). Dwarf scale: a dwarf is 1.3 m, a door 1.5 m.
- **Size**: width × depth × height in metres, measured from the model. **How many**: one of a kind, per room, or a group.
- **Keep clear**: how far it stays from paths, doors and stairs (owner's placement rule, CLAUDE.md).
- **Notes**: lootable (and how much; its `_empty` twin is the looted look), a lid that opens, walk-through, "Heavy
  file" (over about 1 MB), or a clash with the decor rules. "(checked picture)" = the text was checked against the
  model's picture (156 pieces) and corrected where it was wrong.
- Checked by script: all 530 pieces once, every room exists in its dungeon's list, every wall/ceiling piece has a
  height, no "guess" left. Older decor (`night_decor.py`, `furniture.py`, 99 models such as `keg`) is not in this list.

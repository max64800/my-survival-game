# Player reviews: what players love and hate in co-op survival and crafting games

Research date: 2026-11-22. Scope: Steam review themes and player discussion for games like ours. The first-hours and quit-reasons report (`docs/research/players-first-hours-and-why-they-quit.md`) covers onboarding, refunds in the first 2 hours, and a general quit list. This file adds per-game review theme counts, co-op love/hate detail, early-access communication, and the newest 2025-2026 signals. It does not repeat the first-hours material except where noted.

**How sure is this?**
- Most theme counts come from VaporLens (`vaporlens.app`), a site that sorts Steam reviews with software. The tool I used read each page through a small summarising model, so the counts are approximate and I could not check them against raw reviews. Treat counts as "rough rank order," not exact numbers.
- I could not open Steam's "Most Helpful" review pages directly (the store pages are scripted and did not load usable review text). So the "Most Helpful negative reviews" part of the brief is covered only by aggregator themes, not by reading those reviews. This is the main gap.
- Steam review percentages and counts differ between snapshots. I give them as seen, with the date.
- Items marked **(anecdotal)** come from one or a few forum or Steam discussion posts, not from a count.

## Key question 1: Top recurring positive and negative themes per game

### Takeaway
Across the five named games, praise centres on co-op with friends, unique building or exploration, and replayability. Complaints centre on grind or late-game staleness, multiplayer connection problems, performance on big bases or busy caves, weak early guidance, and slow or unclear early-access updates. Co-op is the most-praised theme in almost every game, and co-op connection is the most-complained-about technical theme.

### Cited Findings

**Valheim** (VaporLens summary, Steam, about 69% positive sentiment on the page; Steambase lists about 536k reviews, 504,121 positive and 32,093 negative, with "Very Positive" overall, as of a snapshot):
- Praise: "Great with friends" (27 mentions), "Play with friends recommended" (25), "Highly recommended game" (25), "Varied biomes enhance exploration" (13), "Satisfying progression system" (11), "Excellent survival gameplay" (10). — [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim); totals — [Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/valheim/reviews)
- Complaints: "Extended early access criticized" (25), "Early access incomplete and buggy" (10), "Game is excessively grindy" (10), "Performance varies widely across systems" (10), "Large bases cause FPS drops" (9), "Ashlands biome lags heavily" (6), overheating or crashes (6), "Ore not teleportable via portals" (12), "Death penalty loses skills" (10), Mistlands visibility makes building tedious (10). — [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim)
- Solo play: summaries say solo is possible but less enjoyable. — [Steambase search summary](https://steambase.io/games/valheim/reviews)
- Mistlands (Dec 2025 update, per coverage): a German forum post said "the grind gets bigger, not smaller" with death mechanics; PC Gamer says the developer "heard us loud and clear" about "stingy storage space" and "miserable" navigation of the foggy cliffs, and that storage and navigation were fixed after the public test. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/valheims-massive-mistlands-update-now-live-for-all-players/), [PC Gamer Mistlands review](https://www.pcgamer.com/valheim-mistlands-update-release-date), [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/892970/discussions/0/5143704785442128243) (anecdotal for the forum post)

**Deep Rock Galactic** (VaporLens summary, Steam; the page did not give the overall percentage):
- Praise: co-op multiplayer focus (63 mentions), "fun with friends" (38), "co-op experience excellent" (33), four distinct classes (81), procedural caves for replayability (42), Bosco makes solo viable (56), good performance and optimisation (46), low-end PC performance (20), free seasonal updates (35), DLC purely cosmetic (36), no paywalls or microtransactions (14), friendly community (57). — [VaporLens DRG](https://vaporlens.app/app/548430/deep_rock_galactic)
- Complaints: late-game staleness, "becomes boring after many hours" (23), repetitive missions (13), slow progression and grind, especially overclock unlocks (20), solo "boring and monotonous" (16), network problems: unstable connections, ping spikes, disconnects, host-migration problems (36), stutter and frame drops (17), crashes (17), updates: developers "abandoned" the game, citing the shift to Rogue Core and disappointment with Season 6 (15). — [VaporLens DRG](https://vaporlens.app/app/548430/deep_rock_galactic)

**Core Keeper** (VaporLens analysis page; the page gives the design and refund numbers below):
- Praise: charming pixel-art visuals; "Best experienced with friends" (co-op praised as fun both solo and with friends); cozy builder and decorator archetype as a buy signal; enjoyment (12%), satisfaction (8%). — [VaporLens Core Keeper](https://vaporlens.app/app/1621690/core_keeper/stats/analysis)
- Complaints: "No Tutorial Or Guidance" is the top design complaint at 23.7% of issues and the "most dangerous issue" at 20.7% of churn risk; "Harsh Confusing Start" 2.6%; "Grindy and boring" 5.3%; "Repetitive gameplay loop" 2.6%; "Multiplayer lag and connectivity," "Frequent multiplayer disconnects," and "Poor multiplayer matchmaking" (2.6% each); "Disappearing saves" (2.6%); "Poor class balance" listed; broken controller support (10.5%). — [VaporLens Core Keeper](https://vaporlens.app/app/1621690/core_keeper/stats/analysis)
- Steam listing: "Very Positive," about 20,892 to 23,330 English reviews depending on snapshot. Steam describes 2-8 player drop-in/drop-out online co-op. — [Core Keeper Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1621690/core_keeper/) (via search)

**Enshrouded** (search summaries and existing research; the page did not load for counts):
- Praise: co-op "one of the best experiences"; building is "the most praised system" (repeated praise). Solo described as boring or not recommended by one summary. — [Enshrouded Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1203620/Enshrouded/), [VaporLens Enshrouded](https://vaporlens.app/app/1203620/enshrouded) (search summary). An IGN review is mentioned by a summary, but I did not find or open it.
- Complaints: mid- and late-game grind and repetition; performance (struggles to hold 60 FPS with stutter); too few enemy types; shared quest progression confusion; players expecting guilds or personal bases in a shared world (expectation mismatch). — [VaporLens Enshrouded](https://vaporlens.app/app/1203620/enshrouded) (search summary)

**Grounded** (VaporLens summary, Steam; the URL is for the first Grounded, app 962130):
- Praise: survival and exploration mechanics (99 mentions, most common praise), co-op multiplayer (83; "works well with friends"), storyline (82), gameplay loop (79), crafting and base-building (73), New Game+ replayability (69), "best played with friends" (56). — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded)
- Complaints: solo difficulty (40; "punishing and tedious," "steep spikes," early spiders unfair without a team); multiplayer connectivity (39; disconnects, desyncs); progression unclear and grindy with multi-week timers (36); bugs and crashes (25; crashes 15); performance varies by hardware (14); large bases lag (8), most for the host; unintuitive UI and controls (22); co-op audio issues (7). — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded)
- Grounded 2 (early access, July 2025): mostly positive, complaints centre on performance and crashes and on content depth. One reviewer said enemies catch the player "for seemingly no reason." Obsidian released a stability update but warned it "isn't expected to fix the problems as a whole." — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival/grounded-2-update-improves-the-survival-games-stability-by-around-40-percent-60-percent-while-devs-warn-the-various-performance-improvements-arent-expected-to-fix-the-problems-as-a-whole), [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2661300/Grounded_2/) (search summaries)

**Deep Rock Galactic: Rogue Core** (early access, May 2026; newer spin-off, not the same as Survivor):
- Mixed to "Mostly Positive" at launch (about 2,000 reviews, about 70% positive per one outlet; outlets disagree on the label). Main complaints: the timer (urgency that can feel oppressive), shared progression and shared upgrades that cause friction, co-op friction, and performance. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/fps/deep-rock-galactic-roguelike-spin-off-launches-to-mostly-positive-steam-reviews-that-cant-decide-if-they-love-or-hate-its-timer-and-shared-progression-tool/), [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/desktop-pc/mustafa-mahmoud/deep-rock-galactic-rogue-core-launches-in-early-access-to-mixed-reviews/)

- Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor (Sept 2025, single-player) got favourable critic reviews (Metacritic 86 from 11 critics). I found no player backlash for it. — [Metacritic](https://legacy.prod.metacritic.com/game/pc/deep-rock-galactic-survivor/critic-reviews)

### Inferences
- Co-op with friends is the top praise in five of the six games checked. Our 1-4 player co-op is aimed at the strongest positive theme in the genre.
- Connection and disconnect complaints show up in Grounded (39), Deep Rock Galactic (36), Core Keeper (2.6% each for several connection items), and Rogue Core (co-op friction). Reliable connections are a shared weak spot, not one game's problem.
- Solo play is a frequent complaint in Grounded (40), Deep Rock (16 boring), and Valheim (harder). Deep Rock's "Bosco makes solo viable" (56 mentions) suggests a solo-helper character helps a lot.

### Gaps
- I could not read the "Most Helpful" negative reviews on Steam for any game. The theme counts come from a summariser, not from the reviews themselves.
- I did not find a clean, sourced share of "most helpful" negative reviews per game.
- Enshrouded and Core Keeper counts are partly search summaries; the VaporLens Enshrouded page was not opened for counts.

## Key question 2: Why players stop playing (grind, inventory, weight, boring combat, empty world, repetitive dungeons, building frustration, co-op host problems, performance, save loss)

### Takeaway
The biggest long-term quit reasons are grind and late-game staleness, then multiplayer connection problems, then performance on big bases and busy areas. Logistics (moving ore, storage limits) and harsh solo difficulty also come up often. Save loss is rarer but makes players quit for good.

### Cited Findings
- Grind and late staleness: Deep Rock "becomes boring after many hours" (23), repetitive missions (13), slow overclock unlocks (20) — [VaporLens DRG](https://vaporlens.app/app/548430/deep_rock_galactic). Valheim "excessively grindy" (10) — [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim). Grounded progression "grindy," multi-week timers (36) — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded). Core Keeper "grindy and boring" (5.3%) — [VaporLens Core Keeper](https://vaporlens.app/app/1621690/core_keeper/stats/analysis).
- Logistics and storage: Valheim "ore not teleportable via portals" (12) — [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim). Mistlands "stingy storage space," fixed after the test — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/valheims-massive-mistlands-update-now-live-for-all-players/).
- Death: "death penalty loses skills" (10) — [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim).
- Solo difficulty: Grounded "punishing and tedious," spikes (40) — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded).
- Building and base performance: Valheim large bases cause FPS drops (9) and Mistlands visibility makes building tedious (10) — [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim). Grounded large bases lag, most for the host (8) — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded). Enshrouded large bases lag (search summary) — [VaporLens Enshrouded](https://vaporlens.app/app/1203620/enshrouded).
- Performance and crashes: Deep Rock stutter and frame drops (17), crashes (17) — [VaporLens DRG](https://vaporlens.app/app/548430/deep_rock_galactic). Grounded crashes (15) — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded). Enshrouded struggles to hold 60 FPS (search summary).
- Co-op problems: see question 3.
- Save loss: Core Keeper "disappearing saves" (2.6%) — [VaporLens Core Keeper](https://vaporlens.app/app/1621690/core_keeper/stats/analysis). Anecdotal (Steam discussions): co-op saves only went to the host so joiners lost session progress in Atlas Fallen, and Sons of the Forest saves are written to the host's file, so guests can lose progress — [Atlas Fallen discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1230530/discussions/0/6363075944119256933), [Sons of the Forest discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1326470/discussions/0/3770113546996993110/?ctp=2) (search summaries; I did not open the threads). Earlier research (first-hours file) also notes a Valheim world-destroyer bug and Core Keeper progress vanishing across PC and Steam Deck.
- Refund signal: Core Keeper's refund analysis puts about $103,168 of revenue at risk over Sep 2025 to Sep 2026. Design causes 56.9% ($58,701), technical 37.2% ($38,412), business 5.9% ($6,055). Its caveat: the model's precision was about 88.8%, and the page says churn-risk scores are not churn rates. — [VaporLens Core Keeper](https://vaporlens.app/app/1621690/core_keeper/stats/analysis)

### Inferences
- Design causes outweigh technical causes for refunds in Core Keeper (about 57% vs 37%), but technical causes are still large, and performance complaints keep appearing across all games. Both matter.
- Late-game staleness hurts after many hours. Each new area should bring a new mechanic, not only bigger numbers (matches the first-hours file's point 15).
- Big bases hurt frame rate in several games. Our snapped building needs a performance test with a large base, not only an empty one.

### Gaps
- No source gives a clean share of quits by hours played for these games. The Steam playtime-to-review data is not available through the tools I had (SteamDB pages were not opened).
- Inventory weight limits were not a top theme in any VaporLens page checked. Logistics (ore carrying, storage) is the closer signal.

## Key question 3: What co-op players specifically love and hate

### Takeaway
Players love playing with friends and a friendly community. They hate unstable connections, disconnects, host-only issues, and progress that is shared or split unfairly. Shared timers and shared upgrade pools cause friction (Rogue Core).

### Cited Findings
- Love: co-op is the top praise in Grounded (83), Core Keeper ("best experienced with friends"), Valheim ("great with friends," 27), Deep Rock (co-op 63 mentions, friendly community 57). — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded), [VaporLens Core Keeper](https://vaporlens.app/app/1621690/core_keeper/stats/analysis), [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim), [VaporLens DRG](https://vaporlens.app/app/548430/deep_rock_galactic)
- Hate: multiplayer connection issues in Deep Rock (36), Grounded (39 mentions, "unplayable for some"), Core Keeper (multiplayer lag, disconnects, matchmaking all listed). — same sources
- Host-specific: in Grounded, large-base lag is most noticeable for the host, and connection problems are most frustrating for non-hosts. — [VaporLens Grounded](https://vaporlens.app/app/962130/grounded)
- Shared progress friction: Enshrouded players were irritated that quest progress counted for everyone when one player finished it (search summary) — [VaporLens Enshrouded](https://vaporlens.app/app/1203620/enshrouded). Rogue Core's shared progression and shared upgrades caused friction — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/fps/deep-rock-galactic-roguelike-spin-off-launches-to-mostly-positive-steam-reviews-that-cant-decide-if-they-love-or-hate-its-timer-and-shared-progression-tool/).
- Expectation mismatch: Enshrouded negative reviews came from players expecting guilds or personal bases in a shared world (search summary) — [VaporLens Enshrouded](https://vaporlens.app/app/1203620/enshrouded).
- Drop-in co-op: Core Keeper runs 2-8 players drop-in/drop-out. A GOG reviewer praised it but said it is hard to use on slow or restrictive networks and has no LAN or local co-op — [GOG reviews](https://www.gog.com/u/fanno1113/reviews) (search summary).

### Inferences
- Joining a friend must be fast and reliable. Connection quality is the most common co-op complaint across games, so reconnection, host-migration tests, and a clear connection-quality indicator are worth building early.
- Each player's own progress (own dwarf, own unlocks) avoids the shared-progress friction. Shared world, own rewards.
- Solo should work well. Deep Rock's Bosco-style helper and the Grounded solo complaint both point to giving a solo player a fair path.

### Gaps
- No source gives a count of "host-only world" complaints. The "host-only worlds" split was not measured.
- Late-joiner catch-up was not found as a theme in any source checked. Our game should not assume it is praised or hated.
- Loot-sharing rules were not found as a theme. Anecdotal only.

## Key question 4: Early-access lessons (update cadence and communication)

### Takeaway
Players punish long early-access stretches and slow updates, then reward fast, public responses. Deep Rock's Rogue Core team answered within a day with a roadmap; Valheim's long early access is still a top complaint years later.

### Cited Findings
- Valheim: "Extended early access criticized" (25 mentions) and "early access incomplete and buggy" (10); slow update speed criticised. — [VaporLens Valheim](https://vaporlens.app/app/892970/valheim). Mistlands landed after a long wait; some players questioned the timeline. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/valheim-mistlands-update-new-biome/) (search summary)
- Valheim 2025-2026 developer response: a Steam thread asked Iron Gate to review the Mistlands patch and listen to concerns. Iron Gate fixed storage and navigation complaints from the Mistlands public test. — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/892970/discussions/0/5143704785442128243), [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/valheims-massive-mistlands-update-now-live-for-all-players) (search summary)
- Deep Rock Rogue Core: developers reportedly changed the game in under 24 hours and published a fix plan and roadmap; they prioritised future updates over fixed release dates. — [DLCompare](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/deep-rock-galactic-rogue-core-responds-swiftly-to-community-feedback-77265), [iXBT](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/05/24/razrabotciki-deep-rock-galactic-rogue-core-pererabotaiut-taimer-i-drugie-mexaniki-predstavlen-plan-ispravleniia-igry-i-doroznaia-karta.html)
- Deep Rock (original): free seasonal updates (35 mentions) and cosmetic-only DLC (36) are praised. — [VaporLens DRG](https://vaporlens.app/app/548430/deep_rock_galactic)
- Grounded 2 early access was described as a strong launch, with a published roadmap, and later stability updates were judged as not enough. — [GamesRadar Grounded 2 roadmap](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival/grounded-2-roadmap-promises-new-bosses-buggies-and-more-but-for-now-the-early-access-survival-game-has-already-hit-mostly-positive-steam-reviews-after-crushing-its-predecessors-player-record) (search summary)

### Inferences
- Publishing a roadmap and answering fast seems to protect reviews. Valheim's silence and slow updates are the biggest review complaint. We should set expectations about update timing early and keep them.
- Free updates and cosmetic-only paid content earn praise. Our plan (no paywalls, cosmetic DLC if any) fits this.

### Gaps
- No source measured review score changes against update dates for Valheim or Enshrouded (SteamDB history not opened). Claims about "how much" reviews moved after each update are not sourced.

## Key question 5: Data (review score histories, playtime-to-review, refund-window issues)

### Takeaway
The only hard numbers found are VaporLens review-theme counts and the Core Keeper refund-risk breakdown. No source gave review-score histories or playtime-at-review numbers for these games.

### Cited Findings
- Refund risk, Core Keeper: about $103,168 at risk over Sep 2025 to Sep 2026; design 56.9%, technical 37.2%, business 5.9%; top single issue "No Tutorial Or Guidance" at 23.7% of issues. — [VaporLens Core Keeper](https://vaporlens.app/app/1621690/core_keeper/stats/analysis)
- Valheim totals (approximate, one snapshot): 536,214 reviews, 504,121 positive, 32,093 negative. — [Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/valheim/reviews)
- Enshrouded totals differ by snapshot: 96,039 reviews at 85% positive in one; 48,100 English reviews "Very Positive" in another. — [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1203620/Enshrouded/), [WASDLand](https://wasdland.com/game/enshrouded-1203620) (search summaries)
- Core Keeper English reviews about 20,892 to 23,330 across snapshots. — [Core Keeper Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1621690/core_keeper/) (search)
- Refund window (2 hours, 14 days): already in the first-hours file (TechSpot, FinalBoss).

### Inferences
- The Core Keeper refund split (design first, technical second) suggests the first-hours design work (guidance, first goal) matters more for refunds than raw performance alone, but performance still matters for both.

### Gaps
- No SteamDB score-history data was obtained. Playtime-at-review distribution was not found. Refund rates per game beyond Core Keeper were not found in a sourced form.
- The Steam "Most Helpful" pages were not readable, so the "most helpful negative reviews" check is missing.

## Ranked actions for our game (from this research, cross-checked with the first-hours file)

1. **Reliable co-op joining and reconnection** (connection problems: Grounded 39, Deep Rock 36, Core Keeper several). Test join, leave, and host-migration often. Show connection quality.
2. **Each player keeps their own progress** (shared-progress friction: Enshrouded, Rogue Core). Shared world, own rewards and unlocks.
3. **Solo must be fair** (Grounded solo difficulty 40; Deep Rock's solo helper praised 56). Scale early fights so one dwarf can beat them. Give solo a helper if needed.
4. **Each new area brings a new mechanic** (late staleness: Deep Rock 23, repetitive missions 13, grind across all). Plan a new trick per zone and avoid bigger-numbers-only progress.
5. **Logistics fixes early** (ore carrying, storage limits: Valheim 12 and Mistlands storage complaints). A deposit or send-home option at chests.
6. **Test large bases and busy caves for frame rate** (Valheim 9, Grounded 8, Deep Rock 17 stutter, Enshrouded). Our snapped building is at risk here.
7. **Fair death** (Valheim "death penalty loses skills," 10). Keep penalties clear and recoverable.
8. **Save safety** (disappearing saves 2.6% of Core Keeper issues but high rage; anecdotal host-save losses). Keep the existing autosave plan, and add the "never overwrite the only save" rule from the first-hours file.
9. **Publish a roadmap and answer fast** (Valheim early-access complaints 25; Rogue Core roadmap in under 24 hours). Keep update promises short and public.
10. **No paywalls; cosmetic-only extras** (Deep Rock praise 14 and 36). Fits the free-tools and no-subscription rule.
11. **Give new players a guide, not a lecture** (Core Keeper "no tutorial" 23.7% of issues and top churn risk). Matches the first-hours file's one-line-hints advice.
